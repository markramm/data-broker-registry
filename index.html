Who runs this, and why you can trust it. This tool is made by Transparency Cascade Press, a nonprofit newsroom. It is free and open-source (GPL-3.0). It has no account, no server, and no analytics on what you click here — your choices and progress never leave your browser. We make no money from it and there is nothing to sell, which is the point: a tool like this is only useful if it can't be bought out from under you. (We learned that the hard way — the free app we used to recommend, Permission Slip, was handed to a paid service in May 2026.)

The broker roster comes from Data Brokers Watch (GPL-3.0); opt-out links for the highest-impact brokers are verified by us. For an even fuller by-hand list, see Yael Grauer's "Big Ass Data Broker Opt-Out List." If you're in California, the state's free DROP tool sends one deletion request to every registered broker.
