1. Go to https://homeownersmarketingservices.com/list-removal-request-new/ (HMS's "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" / list-removal form).
2. Enter your full name, current mailing address, and an email address exactly as they would appear on a piece of mail you've received or expect to receive -- HMS states removal only occurs if your name and address are an exact match to a record in their database.
3. Optionally use the comments field to note that you're exercising your CCPA right to opt out of sale/sharing, or to reference a specific mail piece you received.
4. Complete the CAPTCHA and submit the form. No ID upload is required.
5. If you prefer email, send the same information to DoNotMail@HomeownersMarketingServices.com, or call 1-888-888-5557.
6. Note that HMS sources data from public records and sells to other businesses -- a single opt-out with HMS will not necessarily remove you from every downstream mailer who already purchased a list containing your record before your request.
