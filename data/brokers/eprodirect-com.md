2. Fill out the webform: your email address is required, and you can also provide your address (address line 1, city, state, zip code) and website to help EproDirect match your record.
3. Use the checkbox to specify scope -- e.g., "Please do not sell my personal information, however, please include me in your email communications" if you want to stop the sale/rental of your data specifically while still receiving EproDirect's own marketing emails, or leave it unchecked/adjust as offered if you want a broader opt-out.
4. Submit the form.
5. If you'd rather not use the webform, call EproDirect directly at 561-417-5513 and make the same request by phone.
6. If you only want to stop receiving EproDirect's own marketing emails (a narrower request than the data-sale opt-out above), use the unsubscribe link included in any marketing email they send you instead.
