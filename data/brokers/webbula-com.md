1. Go to webbula.com/opt-out-request-d/ (or call 800-533-5853 if you prefer phone).
2. Enter your first name, last name, and email address.
3. Enter your street address (plus unit/apartment if applicable), city, state/province, ZIP/postal code, and country.
4. Optionally add a phone number, then check the consent box and submit.
5. Webbula will attempt to match your submission to records in its database by name and address; allow up to 5 days for processing.
6. Note this only opts out data-append/data-service matching for the identifiers you submit -- for broader ad-targeting opt-outs, also use the NAI or DAA industry opt-out tools or your device's ad-tracking settings.
