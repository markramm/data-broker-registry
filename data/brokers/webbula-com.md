1. Go to https://webbula.com/opt-out-request/ (or call 800-533-5853).
2. Enter your first and last name, your email address (twice), and your street address, city, state, ZIP code and country.
3. Optionally add a phone number, then tick the consent box.
4. Complete the hCaptcha check if one appears, then submit.
5. Webbula matches your request by name and address together, so submit again for every address or name variation you want covered. It may email you to verify. Allow up to 5 days.
6. This only stops Webbula's data-append and data-service matching for what you submit. For ad-targeting opt-outs, also use the NAI or DAA industry opt-out tools or your device's ad-tracking settings.
