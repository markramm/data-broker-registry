1. Go to https://app.retention.com/optout/ ("Retention.com Opt-Out Form"). California residents can use https://app.retention.com/ccpa_details/, which shows the same form.
2. Enter the email address you want removed and your zip code.
3. Complete the "I'm not a robot" CAPTCHA and click "OPT-OUT".
4. Retention.com matches people by email, so repeat the form for every email address you use, especially ones you give to online stores.
5. Alternatively, call 1-(855) 306-2455 and leave a voicemail saying you withdraw consent to the resale of your personal information, giving the email address to opt out.
