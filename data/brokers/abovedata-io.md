1. Go to the [Above Data opt-out request form](https://www.abovedata.io/opt-out).
2. Enter your first name, last name and email address. Company and country are optional.
3. Tick **Opt out of targeted advertising**, and also **Request deletion of information about me** if you want your data removed.
4. Submit the form. It is protected by reCAPTCHA, so you may be asked to solve a challenge.
5. Above Data says it will verify your identity from the information you gave and respond within 30 days. If you can't get through the form, email privacy@abovedata.io with the same request.
