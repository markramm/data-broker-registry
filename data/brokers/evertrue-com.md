2. In the body, give your full name and the email address(es), postal address and school or organization affiliations EverTrue might hold you under (for example, your alma mater and graduation year). Ask EverTrue to delete your personal information and to stop selling or sharing it.
3. EverTrue may reply asking you to verify your identity before it acts. Answer from the same email address.

5. Keep a copy of your email and the date. If you get no response within 45 days, follow up by replying to your original message.
