1. On searchpeoplefree.com, scroll to the footer and click "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" (or go directly to the [opt-out page](https://www.searchpeoplefree.com/opt-out)).
2. Enter your full name and an email address, check the box confirming you own that email, solve the CAPTCHA, and click "Begin removal process."
3. Open the email SearchPeopleFree sends you and click "Validate My Email."
4. On the page that follows, search your name and location again, find your matching record, and click "Continue Removal," then "Remove this record."
5. Clear your browser cache and re-check after about 72 hours to confirm the listing is gone.
6. If you have more than one profile (e.g., from a previous address), repeat the process for each.
