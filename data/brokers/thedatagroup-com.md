1. Go to The Data Group's privacy page at https://thedatagroup.com/privacy/.
2. Use the "I am a California resident do not sell my personal data" link in the footer, or the Consumer Privacy Request Form referenced on that page, to submit an opt-out request.
3. Alternatively, email compliance@thedatagroup.com directly with your full name and the email address(es) you want opted out, stating you are requesting deletion/opt-out of sale under CCPA (or your applicable state law).
4. The company states it will honor opt-out requests within 45 days; watch for a confirmation reply.
5. The Data Group also lists opt-out coverage for residents of CO, CT, DE, IN, IA, MT, NJ, FL, OR, NE, TN, TX, UT, VA, VT, and NH -- mention your state if you live in one of these and the standard process doesn't seem to apply.
