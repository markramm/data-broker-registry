1. Go to the [Amobee Consumer Opt Out page](https://www.amobee.com/trust/consumer-opt-out/) and follow the browser-based opt-out instructions; this ties the opt-out to your Amobee cookie ID or mobile device ID rather than your name.
2. For a formal CCPA data access, deletion, or opt-out-of-sale request, use the [CCPA request portal](https://www.amobee.com/ccpa/) or email amobeeprivacy@amobee.com.
3. You can also call +1.877.326.5657 with questions about your request.
4. Save any case number you receive -- Amobee's portal reportedly only shows it once and requests can take up to 30 days to process.
5. Note that browser-based opt-outs (cookies) can be lost if you clear cookies or switch browsers/devices, so you may need to repeat the opt-out periodically.
