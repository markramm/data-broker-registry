1. Go to the [Enformion opt-out portal](https://www.enformion.com/opt-out/) -- Confi-Chek shares its data and opt-out process with Enformion and has no separate removal form of its own.
2. Enter your first, middle (optional), and last name and your email address. If you're acting as an authorized agent for someone else, use the agent fields instead.
3. Complete the reCAPTCHA checkbox and submit.
4. Check your email within 24 hours for a follow-up link -- it expires after that window.
5. Click the link and re-enter your information exactly as before so it matches the original request.
6. Allow about 3 days for Enformion (and therefore Confi-Chek) to fully remove your record.
