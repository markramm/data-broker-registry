1. Go to https://www.californiaengineering.org/ and search for your own name to find your license listing.
2. Open your listing and note the record ID shown in the bottom-right corner of the license-details block.
3. Email removals@licensedata.org with the record ID and your full name as shown on the listing, requesting removal from californiaengineering.org.
4. The site notes that delisting may make it harder for others to verify your license through this particular unofficial site -- your official status remains independently verifiable through the California Board for Professional Engineers, Land Surveyors, and Geologists.
5. This site is one of several run by the same operator (californiabrokers.org, californialicensing.org, californianursing.org, californiapharmacists.org, and others in the same footer network) -- if you hold more than one license type, check whether a matching listing exists on the related sites and repeat the removal request there too.
