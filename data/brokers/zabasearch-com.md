1. Go to intelius.com/opt-out and search your name plus state to locate the record feeding your ZabaSearch listing.
2. Select the matching listing and follow the prompts to submit a removal request.
3. Check your email for the PeopleConnect verification message and click "Verify Email."
4. Enter your date of birth so PeopleConnect can confirm it has matched the correct record.
5. Submit and allow 3-7 days for processing after email verification.
6. Afterward, search zabasearch.com, addresses.com, and anywho.com separately to confirm the listing is gone from each -- these PeopleConnect-affiliated sites can carry duplicate records that need their own removal requests.
