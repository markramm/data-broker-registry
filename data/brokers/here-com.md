1. Go to https://here.com/en-gb/privacy/do-not-sell (HERE's "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" page).
2. Locate and open the Data Subject Request Form linked from that page, and select the option to opt out of sale/sharing of your personal information.
3. Provide the requested identifying details (name, email, and the region/jurisdiction you're submitting under) so HERE can locate any records associated with you.
4. Separately, to stop browser/cookie-based tracking on here.com, click "Cookie Preferences" in the site footer and adjust your consent settings.
5. If the request form is unavailable or you need broader detail, email privacy@here.com directly and reference the U.S. State Privacy Laws Supplement mentioned in HERE's privacy policy.
6. Watch for a confirmation and keep a copy of your submission.
