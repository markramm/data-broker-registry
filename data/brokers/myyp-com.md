1. Go to optoutyp.com (the current home of the Yellow Pages directory opt-out, formerly at yellowpagesoptout.com).
2. Complete the reCAPTCHA challenge on the page.
3. Fill in your name, email, and full address (address, city, state, zip are all required) and submit the form to stop physical yellow-pages-book delivery to your address.
4. This form only stops directory delivery -- it does not remove your name/address/phone listing itself. To do that, identify which publisher covers your area (Pinnacle, Pioneer Telephone Association, or Thryv/The Real Yellow Pages) and contact that publisher directly using the email or phone number listed on the opt-out site.
5. If you're specifically trying to remove a myyp.com online listing (not just stop book delivery), search for your listing on myyp.com first and use the publisher contact info to request its removal explicitly.
