1. Go to the [LSEG opt-out-of-sale web form](https://privacyportalde-cdn.onetrust.com/dsarwebform/5f7a2da0-bed0-45e8-ac2c-c1f297e2efdc/1234b4c9-a981-4092-b398-cb80005998cf.html) (hosted on OneTrust; reached via the "Opt-out of the sale of your personal information online" link on LSEG's California Consumer Privacy Notice page).
2. Fill in your name and email address and specify that you are requesting to opt out of the sale/sharing of your personal information under the CCPA.
3. Submit the form; LSEG states it may contact you to verify your identity before fulfilling the request.
4. Separately, to stop the cookie-level tracking/advertising "sale," visit LSEG's cookie preference centre (linked from the same privacy notice) and opt out of advertising cookies on lseg.com and refinitiv.com.
5. If you prefer not to submit online, call LSEG at +1-866-463-4531, or email privacy.enquiries@lseg.com for a Shine-the-Light disclosure request.
6. Note that clearing your browser cookies after opting out will remove your cookie-level opt-out preference, requiring you to repeat step 4.
