1. Go to the [Resonate Do Not Sell My Personal Information page](https://www.resonate.com/ccpa-do-not-sell/).
2. Fill in the requested fields (typically full name and email address) to identify your record.
3. Submit the request; you may also use the [general CCPA consumer request form](https://www.resonate.com/ccpa-consumer-request/) if you want to request access to or deletion of your data in addition to opting out of sale.
4. Alternatively, email privacy@resonate.com or call 888-237-9353 to submit the same request without using the web form.
5. For broader ad-tracking opt-outs beyond Resonate specifically, consider also using the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out tool at aboutads.info/choices and, for mobile apps, youradchoices.com/appchoices.
6. Resonate's policy states these rights apply to residents of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, and Utah; other states may not receive a response.
