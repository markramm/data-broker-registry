1. Go to `https://www.intelius.com/optout` -- this is the shared suppression portal that also covers publicrecords.com listings (PeopleConnect network).
2. Enter your first and last name and select the state where your listing appears, then search.
3. Find your record in the results and select it to start a removal request.
4. Provide your email address and solve the CAPTCHA challenge, then submit.
5. Check your email for a confirmation message from Intelius and click the verification link to finalize the request.
6. Allow up to 72 hours for the listing to disappear from publicrecords.com; if it doesn't, call 206-301-5700 and reference the suppression request.
