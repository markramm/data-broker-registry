1. Go to the Yardi privacy-request/feedback form at https://www.yardi.com/about-us/privacy-statement-feedback/ (PropertyShark is a Yardi-owned brand and privacy requests route through Yardi).
2. State that you are a California resident (or otherwise covered) exercising your right to opt out of the sale of your personal information as it appears on PropertyShark, and identify the property/listing showing your name.
3. Alternatively, email support@propertyshark.com directly with the same request, including your full name and the property address in question.
4. Or call 1-800-866-1124, or mail a written request to: Attn: Privacy Manager, Yardi Systems, 430 S. Fairview, Santa Barbara, CA 93117, USA.
5. Expect a response within about 45 days per CCPA timelines.
6. Note that most of the personal information PropertyShark/Yardi holds is drawn from public government property records or maintained on behalf of Yardi's business clients -- removal from PropertyShark's own listing does not remove the underlying record from the county/municipal source it was drawn from.
