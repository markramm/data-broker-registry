3. If Claritas cannot match your request to their offline marketing database from the online form alone, they may direct you to email privacyinfo@claritas.com (or write to Privacy Officer, Claritas LLC, 8044 Montgomery Road Suite 455, Cincinnati OH 45236) and attach a copy of a valid state ID or a utility bill to verify your identity.

5.
