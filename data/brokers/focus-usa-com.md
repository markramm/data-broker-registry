1. Go to the [Focus USA Your Privacy Choices page](https://www.focus-usa.com/your-privacy-choices/).
2. Enter your first and last name, street address, city, state and ZIP, and your email address (twice). Focus USA matches records by name and address or by email, so add any other email addresses and phone numbers you have used in the optional fields.
3. Answer "No" to "Are you submitting request on behalf of someone?" if the request is for yourself.
4. Under "Your Privacy Choices", tick **Opt-Out**, and also **Delete** if you want your record removed rather than just suppressed from sale.
5. Solve the simple arithmetic question at the bottom of the form and submit.
6. If the form does not work for you, email privacy@focus-usa.com with the same information and say you are opting out of the sale and sharing of your personal information.
