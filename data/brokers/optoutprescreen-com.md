1. Go to the official site at [optoutprescreen.com](https://www.optoutprescreen.com/) -- verify the URL carefully, as scam sites mimic this domain, and note the real service never calls or emails you first.
2. Choose to opt out (rather than opt in) of prescreened credit and insurance offers.
3. For the quick 5-year electronic opt-out, submit your name, address, date of birth, and Social Security number online (this data is used only to match you against the credit bureaus' prescreening lists) -- or call 1-888-567-8688 (1-888-5-OPTOUT) to do the same by phone.
4. If you want a permanent opt-out instead of the 5-year term, start the request online or by phone, then print, sign, and mail in the confirmation form the site provides -- permanent opt-out cannot be completed electronically alone.
5. There is no fee either way; this stops prescreened offers, not all marketing mail, so pair it with DMAchoice if you also want to cut down on general marketing mail.
6. The 5-year opt-out will lapse -- if you chose that option, calendar a reminder to renew, or just do the permanent mail-in version once to avoid repeating this.
