1. Note that Retargetly was acquired by Epsilon (a Publicis Groupe company); retargetly.com itself no longer hosts a working site, and rights requests are handled through Epsilon's shared privacy portal.
2. Go to [Epsilon's Consumer Privacy Rights request form](https://legal.epsilon.com/dsr).
3. Select "United States" as your country and fill in the requested fields (typically full name and email address).
4. Specify that your request concerns Retargetly / Epsilon's data management and audience-segmentation services, and select the appropriate request type (opt-out of sale/sharing, access, or deletion).
5. Submit the form. Epsilon states it may keep a record of the request and response as required by law, and that additional proof is required if you are submitting on behalf of someone else.
6. If you prefer not to use the online form, call Epsilon's US consumer line at +1-866-267-3861.
7. For background on what data is collected, see [Retargetly's privacy policy](https://legal.epsilon.com/retargetly/privacy-policy-services), which describes probabilistic/deterministic audience data including geolocation and purchase-intent inferences.
