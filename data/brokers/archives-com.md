1. Go directly to the [Archives.com opt-out form](https://www.archives.com/optout) -- you do not need to create an account or start a free trial to submit this form, even though searching the site for your own listing will try to push you into a paid trial signup.
2. Fill in the identifying information you want suppressed: first and last name are required, plus at least one address line; phone and email for that listing are optional but help match the right record.
3. Fill in your own contact details as the requester (name, email, entered twice to confirm), and in the "additional information" box briefly note how you found the listing (e.g., "found via Archives.com people search for [name] in [city, state]").
4. Solve the CAPTCHA and check the box agreeing to the opt-out policy, then submit.
5. Watch your email for Archives.com's confirmation once they've identified and suppressed the matching record -- allow up to 30 days for processing.
6. If you have multiple listings (e.g., under a maiden name or old address), submit a separate request for each one.
