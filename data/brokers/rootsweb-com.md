1. RootsWeb is owned by Ancestry.com and operates under Ancestry's privacy policy and legal terms, not a separate RootsWeb-specific policy.
2. For the ad-targeting/data-sale opt-out that Ancestry offers, go to https://www.ancestry.com/legal/ccpa-donotshare-sell (also linked as "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" in the footer of Ancestry and RootsWeb sites) and follow the on-page instructions.
3. If your browser or device has Global Privacy Control (GPC) enabled, Ancestry states it will honor that signal automatically.
4. This opt-out only stops use of your data for targeted advertising -- it does NOT remove your name from RootsWeb message-board posts, mailing-list archives, or WorldConnect family trees that other users have submitted mentioning you.
5. If you are named in a RootsWeb-hosted message board post, mailing list archive, or someone else's family tree and want that content removed or corrected, you will need Ancestry's separate user-generated-content takedown/support process; this was not confirmed during this research pass, so contact Ancestry support directly and reference the specific RootsWeb page URL containing your information.
6. Because RootsWeb pages are static/archived in many cases, removal of user-submitted content may require contacting the original submitter or Ancestry support rather than a self-service form.
