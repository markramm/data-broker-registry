1. Find the listing you want removed on buzzfile.com and copy its exact URL (e.g. a company or contact profile page).
2. Go to https://www.buzzfile.com/Company/Remove and follow the on-page instructions -- this may require registering or claiming the listing to submit the removal request.
3. If the removal form does not work or you cannot complete registration, email sales@buzzfile.com directly, include the listing URL, and explicitly request removal of your personal/contact information.
4. Buzzfile does not publish a detailed written opt-out policy, so keep a copy of your request and follow up if you don't see the listing removed within a couple of weeks.
5. Because Buzzfile draws from public business records, expect it may need to be repeated if your information is re-added from a later data refresh.
