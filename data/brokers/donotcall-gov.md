1. Go to https://www.donotcall.gov/register/reg.aspx (or call 1-888-382-1222 / TTY 1-866-290-4236 to register by phone instead).
2. Enter the phone number(s) you want to register (up to three at a time) and your email address.
3. Click Submit, then review and confirm your information on the next screen and click Register.
4. Check your email -- the FTC sends a confirmation message for each number, and you must click the link in it within 72 hours to complete registration.
5. Your number appears on the Registry the next day, but expect it to take up to 31 days before telemarketing calls actually stop.
6. Registration does not expire and does not need to be renewed; it stops telemarketing sales calls but not calls from political campaigns, charities, surveys, or companies you already do business with.
