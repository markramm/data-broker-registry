1. Go to https://www.peopledatalabs.com/do-not-sell-or-share.
2. Enter your full name, pick your U.S. state or territory of residence, and enter your email address.
3. Optionally add more identifiers (for example other emails, phone numbers or a LinkedIn URL) so PDL can match more of its records about you. The opt-out only covers records it can match to what you enter.
4. Tick the three acknowledgement boxes and click "Submit opt-out request".
5. This form only opts you out of sale, sharing and targeted advertising, and is for U.S. residents. To have your data deleted, accessed or corrected, or if you live outside the U.S., use the "Go to Privacy Request Center" button on the same page, or email privacy@peopledatalabs.com.
