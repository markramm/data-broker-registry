1. Go to beenverified.com/app/optout/search and search for your own listing by name and state (or paste the exact listing URL for your Number Guru/BeenVerified profile if you already have it).
2. Locate your specific record in the search results and select "This is me" (or the equivalent opt-out link) next to it.

5. Check your email and click the confirmation link to finalize the removal request.
