1. Go to intelius.com/opt-out and search for your name plus state to locate your Intelius/US Search listing.
2. Select the listing that matches you and follow the prompt to submit a removal request.
3. Check your email for a verification message from PeopleConnect and click the "Verify Email" button.
4. Enter your date of birth when prompted so PeopleConnect can confirm it's matching the right record.
5. Submit the request and allow 3-7 days for processing after email verification.
6. Search intelius.com and ussearch.com again afterward to confirm the listing is gone -- removal from one PeopleConnect brand does not automatically remove you from the others.
