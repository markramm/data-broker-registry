1. Go to usa-people-search.com/manage and enter your email address.
2. Read and agree to the Terms and Conditions, then complete the "I'm not a robot" CAPTCHA challenge.
3. Enter your first name, last name, city, and state in the search bar and click Search.
4. Find your listing in the results, click "View Details" to confirm it's you, then click "Remove Record."
5. Check your email for the confirmation link from usa-people-search.com and click it to finalize the request.
6. Allow up to 72 hours for the listing to disappear from search results.
