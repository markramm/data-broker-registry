1. Go to https://swoop.com/your-privacy-choices/ and find the opt-out form under "Your Privacy Rights".
2. In the embedded privacy portal, confirm your country and choose your state.
3. Choose "Opt-Out Request" and fill in the details it asks for, usually your email address. Swoop says it only keeps de-identified consumer data, so it forwards your request to the data onboarders it works with.
4. Submit, and watch your inbox for a follow-up.
5. If the portal doesn't work, email the same request to privacyrights@swoop.com.
6. Turning on Global Privacy Control in your browser also tells Swoop's websites not to sell or share your data.
