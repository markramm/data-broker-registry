Phone Registry is a reverse-phone/people-search site; treat it like any people-search opt-out.

1. Go to `http://www.phoneregistry.com/privacy.php` and use your browser's find-on-page for "opt out" to jump to the opt-out section (anchored at `#opt`).
2. Locate your listing first -- search your own name or phone number on the site so you have the exact listing URL to reference.
3. Fill in the opt-out form with your name and the phone number/listing you want suppressed.
4. Submit the form. If no confirmation appears, or the online form doesn't work, call 800-309-9351 or write to their mailing address (P.O. Box 990043, Boston, MA 02199) and ask them to suppress your listing under state privacy law.
5. Re-check the listing after a few days; if it reappears, resubmit -- broker databases refresh periodically.
