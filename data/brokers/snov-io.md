1. Go to https://snov.io/do-not-sell-my-personal-information.
2. Enter your first and last name, the email address you want covered, and your full postal address (street, city, state, postal code, country).
3. Choose "Do not sell my personal information" as the requested action, complete the CAPTCHA check, and submit.
4. Snov.io emails you a confirmation code. Enter it in the field that appears on the page and click Verify.
5. To have an email address removed from Snov.io's lookup results, also try its clear-email tool at https://app.snov.io/clear-email (linked as "Claim" in the site footer).
6. If you're acting for someone else, or the form fails, email help@snov.io.
