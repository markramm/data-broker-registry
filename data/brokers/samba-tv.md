1. If you have a TV with Samba TV software, open the TV's Settings menu and find "Samba Interactive TV". Switch it off to stop viewing-data collection. If you'd rather keep it on, turn off "Ad Personalization" (on some TVs: turn on "Limit Ad Tracking") and reset the device ID. Menu names vary by TV brand.
2. If you installed a Samba TV mobile app, delete it.
3. To also have Samba stop selling or processing data it already holds, go to https://samba.com/privacy-center/notice-of-right-to-opt-out and open the "web form" link under "Opt-out Using the Web Form".
4. Choose "Myself", then "Do Not Sell My Information". Submit the form again with "Delete My Information" if you also want deletion. Answer whether you have a Samba-enabled TV.
5. Enter your first name, last name, email and phone number. Solve the image CAPTCHA and submit.
6. Watch for Samba's email asking you to verify your identity, and follow it. You can appeal a refusal through the same email ticket.
