1. Go to https://www.lusha.com/privacy-center/request-removal/ (the "Opt Out" link in Lusha's site footer). The form loads in a frame on that page.
2. Choose why you reached the page (you want your contact details removed), pick your country of residence and, if you're in the U.S., your state.
3. Enter the email address you want suppressed. Lusha's database is built around work emails and phone numbers, so start with your work email. Submit a separate request for each address.
4. Complete the "I'm not a robot" CAPTCHA and submit.
5. Watch for an email from Lusha asking you to confirm that you own the address, and follow it.
6. If the form doesn't work, email privacy@lusha.com asking to be removed from the Lusha B2B database and added to its suppression list, or (California residents) call 1-866-467-8688 and enter service code 2001#.
