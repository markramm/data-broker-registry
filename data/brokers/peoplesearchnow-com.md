1. Go to peoplesearchnow.com/opt-out.
2. Check the two boxes agreeing to the removal terms and conditions, enter your email address, complete the CAPTCHA, and click "Opt Out My Info."
3. Separately, search the site for your own record using your name, phone number, or address.
4. Open the matching record, click "View All Info" to confirm it's you, then click "Remove Record."
5. Check your email for a message from PeopleSearchNow and click the confirmation link.
6. Allow up to 72 hours for the record to be removed; fill in the form accurately, since incomplete details can stall the request.
