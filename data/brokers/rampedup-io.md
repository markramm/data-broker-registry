1. Go to the RampedUp Privacy Center at https://basic.rampedup.io/app/donotsell and stay on the "Opt Out Request" tab.
2. Enter the email address whose record you want removed, and pick your country. Both are required. Phone and name are optional.
3. Tick the reCAPTCHA checkbox and submit. RampedUp says this one request opts you out of sale and sharing and deletes any record it holds.
4. Repeat for each work or personal email address that might be in its database.
5. If the form doesn't work for you, write to contact@rampedup.io or call (888) 278-3433 and ask for the same opt-out and deletion.
