1. Cell Phone Registry's listings are served through InfoTracer's shared opt-out portal -- go to [infotracer.com/optout](https://infotracer.com/optout/).
2. Enter your first name, last name, state, and city, then complete the CAPTCHA to view search results.
3. Find your record in the list and check its "select record" box, then click the "Remove Data" button that appears.
4. Enter your email address and complete the CAPTCHA again, then submit.
5. Check your inbox for a confirmation email and click the verification link inside it to finalize the removal.
6. Allow up to 72 hours for the record to be removed.
