1. Email support@libertydata.net and ask to opt out / be removed from Libertydata.Net's people-search database. Include your full name and enough identifying detail (e.g. city/state, or a link to your specific listing if you found one) for them to locate your record.
2. Alternatively, call 800-299-8280 and make the same request by phone.
3. Libertydata's own privacy policy states you can opt out "at any time" through either channel, with no form, fee, or ID requirement described.
4. Keep a copy of your email as a timestamped record of the request, since Libertydata says it has not registered as a data broker in the states that require it and does not claim FCRA coverage -- there's less regulatory backstop here than with larger brokers, so documentation helps if you need to follow up.
5. Watch for the listing to reappear; smaller, loosely-regulated people-search sites like this one frequently re-scrape public records without a fixed schedule.
