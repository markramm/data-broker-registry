1. Go to the [Data Decisions Group privacy preferences page](https://datadecisionsgroup.com/preferences/). A "Consumer Privacy Rights" panel should open; if it doesn't, use the "Privacy Choices" link in the site footer.
2. Click the **Data Subject Rights** tab. The panel says this tab may not appear for visitors outside the United States.
3. Under "Select a Request Type", choose **Delete your data**.
4. Fill in your first and last name, email, country, street address, city, state and postal code, and choose the option that describes you under "I am a (an)". Request details are optional; you can add that you also want your data excluded from future sales.
5. Submit the form. It uses reCAPTCHA, so you may be asked to solve a challenge.
6. DDG says it may ask for more details to verify you and aims to answer within 45 days. If you'd rather not use the form, call 1-888-530-8596.
