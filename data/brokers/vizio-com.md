1. If you sign in to your VIZIO TV or apps with a Walmart account, make your request through Walmart's "Your Privacy Choices" form instead; it covers both VIZIO and Walmart data. Otherwise, continue below.
2. Open VIZIO's privacy request form at https://privacyportal-cdn.onetrust.com/dsarwebform/41aebd10-58ee-4c98-9dd5-aa66c9be664a/0099df9c-c0aa-4bf7-bd5f-7f4ff46768f6.html (or call 1-855-472-8817).
3. Select your country and state. Choose the requests your state offers, such as opting out of sale/sharing and deleting your data.
4. Enter the email address VIZIO would have on file for you, solve the reCAPTCHA and submit.
5. Open the identity-confirmation email VIZIO sends and confirm. VIZIO won't process the request otherwise.
6. On the TV itself, look in the privacy or admin settings for a "Viewing Data" (automatic content recognition) setting and turn it off, so new viewing data isn't collected. Menu names vary by model.
