1. Email support@publicrecord.com. (If you'd rather use a form, the contact form is at https://publicrecord.com/contact/. The site may first show a Cloudflare "verify you are human" check.)
2. Say that you are opting out of the sale and sharing of your personal information and asking for it to be deleted. Ask for your profile to be removed from PublicRecord.com's search results as well. If you live in California, say you are making the request under the CCPA.
3. Give your full name and your email address so the company can find and verify your record. If you can see your profile on the site, include the city and state it shows. Don't send ID or other sensitive data unless they ask for it and you're comfortable providing it.
4. Reply to any verification message. PublicRecord.com says it denies requests it cannot verify.
5. PublicRecord.com reports a median response time of under 9 days for opt-outs. After a couple of weeks, search your name on the site again, and follow up by email if your listing still appears.
