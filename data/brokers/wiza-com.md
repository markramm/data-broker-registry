2. Enter your first and last name and your main email address.
3. Wiza recommends adding both your work and personal email addresses and any current or past phone numbers, so its match catches every record. Use the optional second email and phone fields for that.
4. Complete the "I'm not a robot" CAPTCHA and click "Submit my request."
5. If the form doesn't work, or you have more than two emails or phone numbers, email optout@wiza.com asking that your contact information be removed and no longer provided to Wiza's users.
