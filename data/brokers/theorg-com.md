1. Go to https://theorg.com/opt-out and click "Continue".
2. In "Search position", find the position (your name and job at a company) that you want removed, and select it.
3. When asked, give your work email address at that company and confirm it from the email The Org sends. The Org says a personal or anonymous email address may not work, because the work email is how it identifies positions.
4. Repeat for each position listed under your name, such as past jobs.
5. If you no longer have that work email, or the flow fails, email privacy@theorg.com asking them to remove your profile and stop selling or sharing your information, with the link to the profile page.
6. Check back later: The Org says listings are crowdsourced and may be added again. It keeps your work email to try to prevent that.
