1. Go to https://consumers.clarityservices.com/prescreenOffer to reach Clarity Services' online prescreen opt-out tool.
2. Follow the prompts to opt out of unsolicited prescreened credit/insurance offers -- you'll typically need to confirm your name and mailing address.
3. Choose between opting out for 5 years or opting out permanently.
4. If you prefer not to use the online portal, call Clarity's Consumer Support team at 1-866-390-3118 (Mon-Fri, 9am-5pm EST) and request the same opt-out over the phone.
5. Alternatively, mail or fax a completed Prescreen Opt-Out Request Form to Clarity Services, Inc., a part of Experian, Consumer Support Division, PO Box 16, Allen, TX 75013.
6. This opt-out stops prescreened offers generated from Clarity's data; it does not delete your underlying credit file -- if you want your file suppressed or corrected, use Clarity's separate disclosure/dispute process referenced on their support pages.
