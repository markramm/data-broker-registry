1. Search for yourself on peoplebyname.com and open your matching listing.
2. Copy the 9-digit Record ID shown on the listing page.
3. Go to the removal form at peoplebyname.com/remove.php and enter your full name, an email address you can check, and the Record ID.
4. Enter a removal reason (any text works, e.g. "privacy concerns").
5. Complete the "I'm not a robot" CAPTCHA and submit the request.
6. Watch for a confirmation email; removal is typically completed within about 24 hours, but check back periodically since re-scraped public records can reappear.
