1. Search spyfly.com for your own name to confirm whether they actually have a listing on you -- don't submit personal details through the removal form unless you've confirmed a match, since SpyFly is a paid people-search product.
2. If you find a listing, go to the [removal form](https://www.spyfly.com/help-center/remove-my-public-record), enter your first name, last name, age, and state, and solve the CAPTCHA.
3. When your record appears, click "Remove," then enter an email address (consider a secondary one) and check the required boxes.
4. Check that inbox for SpyFly's verification email and click the confirmation link to complete the request.
5. If the online form doesn't turn up your record or doesn't work, email support@spyfly.com directly with your first/last name, age, current and previous address, and email, and ask them to remove your listing.
6. SpyFly's public-record feeds refresh periodically, so recheck every couple of months and repeat the process if your listing reappears.
