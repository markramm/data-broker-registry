1. Go to https://disqus.com/data-sharing-settings/ ("Privacy Choices"). You don't need a Disqus account.
2. In the Cookies section, click "OPT OUT" to stop Disqus collecting cookie data for targeted advertising in this browser. Repeat on each browser and device you use.
3. Under "How To Make a Request", click the link to open Disqus's privacy request form.
4. Choose "Disqus User" if you have ever had an account, otherwise "Other". Tick "Do not sell or share my information" and "Delete my personal information".
5. Enter your first and last name, the email address Disqus may know you by, and your country and state. Leave the details box free of extra personal information. Complete the reCAPTCHA and click "Submit".
6. Open the verification email Disqus sends and confirm the request.
7. If you have never had a Disqus account, Disqus says its data about you is likely limited to advertising cookies. Also use the industry opt-out at https://optout.aboutads.info/ from each browser.
8. Alternatives: call Disqus's automated line at 855-906-0569, or email privacy@disqus.com with the same request.
