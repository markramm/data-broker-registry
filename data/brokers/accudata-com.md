1. Go to https://privacy.deepsync.com/ (Deep Sync is the current parent brand for AccuData Integrated Marketing; accudata.com's privacy policy redirects here).
2. Click "Do Not Sell My Personal Information."
3. Choose "Myself" from the "Who is Opting out?" dropdown, then enter your first and last name, email address, and mailing address.
4. Check the boxes to opt out of sale/sharing and, if desired, to request deletion of your data.
5. Complete the CAPTCHA and submit the form.
6. Alternatively, email privacy.compliance@deepsync.com directly with the same request.
7. Allow up to 45 business days for the request to be processed.
