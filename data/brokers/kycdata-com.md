1. Email consumers@kycdata.com and state you are exercising your CCPA right to opt out of the sale of your personal information (include the phrase "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" and, if you want, request deletion).
2. Include your full name and mailing address so KYC Data can locate your homeowner/mortgage record.
3. Alternatively, visit https://consumer.kycdata.com to view what KYC Data has compiled about you and suppress it directly -- you will need to confirm your identity via an email link before you can view or suppress the record.
4. Watch for a confirmation email and follow any link it contains to complete the process.
5. Keep a copy of your email or portal confirmation for your records; re-check periodically since new homeowner/mortgage records are added weekly.
