1. Determine whether your case qualifies: BustedMugshots.com states it will only remove listings where the case was sealed, expunged, you were found not guilty, the charges were dismissed, or the subject is deceased. It does not offer general removal and does not accept payment for removal.
2. Gather supporting documentation proving your case qualifies -- e.g. a court order of expungement/sealing, a dismissal record, or (for a deceased subject) a certified death certificate.
3. Email help@bustedmugshots.com with your full name, a link to your specific listing on the site, and the supporting documentation attached.
4. Keep your request factual, polite, and non-threatening -- removal in qualifying cases is reportedly handled at the site's discretion.
5. If your case does not meet one of these narrow criteria, BustedMugshots.com is unlikely to remove your listing directly; consider a broader reputation-management/suppression strategy instead.
6. Because BustedMugshots.com is only one of many mugshot-aggregator sites, a successful removal here will not remove copies of the same mugshot from other, separately operated mugshot sites -- each requires its own request.
