1. Go to https://legal.epsilon.com/dsr/, Epsilon's Consumer Privacy Request portal.
2. Select your country/region (US consumers should pick United States).
3. Fill out the Consumer Privacy Request Form with your name, email, and mailing address, and specify that you want to opt out of / delete your marketing data.
4. Submit the form and complete any verification step it presents.
5. If you're acting as an authorized agent on someone else's behalf, be ready to provide written proof of authority -- Epsilon requires this for third-party requests.
6. Alternatively, call 1-866-267-3861 to submit the same request by phone. Epsilon employees with privacy questions should instead email privacyofficer@publicisgroupe.com.
