1. Know that SearchQuarry no longer runs its own name-search opt-out form: its search results now come from TruthFinder and BeenVerified, and removing yourself from those results has to be done on their sites. SearchQuarry's [Your Privacy Choices](https://www.searchquarry.com/privacy-choices/) page links both.
2. Opt out at TruthFinder: go to https://www.truthfinder.com/opt-out/ and follow the steps in the TruthFinder entry (CAPTCHA and email verification).
3. Opt out at BeenVerified: go to https://www.beenverified.com/svc/optout/search/, search your name and state, select your listing, and confirm through the email BeenVerified sends.
4. To delete anything SearchQuarry itself holds (search terms, referral records, or a members-area account), email privacy@searchquarry.com saying which right you are using (for example, deletion or opt-out of sale/sharing). You can also call (800) 859-7375 or write to Search Quarry, LLC, 732 S 6th St, Ste R, Las Vegas, NV 89101. Expect a reply within 45 days; they may ask you to confirm your identity.
5. If you are a judge, prosecutor, law enforcement officer, or a household family member of one, email privacy@searchquarry.com with your full name, residence, email address, and covered-person status, using the subject line "Attention Covered Person Removal Request." Contact TruthFinder and BeenVerified as well, since this request only covers SearchQuarry's own records.
6. Optionally, turn off "Allow advertising tags" on the same page (or use a browser that sends Global Privacy Control) to stop ad tracking in that browser.
