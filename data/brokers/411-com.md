1. Go to [411.com](https://www.411.com) and search your own name plus city/state or ZIP to find your listing.
2. Open your listing's detail page and copy its full URL from the address bar (411.com listings are served by Whitepages, so removal happens on Whitepages, not on 411.com itself).
3. Go to the [Whitepages suppression request page](https://www.whitepages.com/suppression-requests) and paste in your listing URL.
4. Confirm the listing shown is yours, pick a reason for removal from the dropdown, and enter a phone number where you can be reached right now.
5. Answer the automated verification call and enter the code it reads out into the on-screen field, then complete the CAPTCHA if prompted.
6. Submit the request. Removal is not instant -- check back periodically, since Whitepages/411 listings can reappear after new data pulls.
