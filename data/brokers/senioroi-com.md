1. Go to https://senioroi.com/privacy-request/ (SenioROI's "Submit a Privacy Rights Request" page). The form loads in a frame on that page.
2. Enter your email, your full legal name and your state. Choose that the request is for yourself, and under "What would you like us to do" choose to stop selling or sharing your information (submit again and choose delete if you want that too).
3. SenioROI matches records by name, address and phone number, so fill in your current mailing address and phone. List previous addresses, other phone numbers and other email addresses from the past several years in their boxes.
4. Tick the box declaring the information is accurate and that you're the person named, then submit.
5. Watch your email: SenioROI says it may write back with questions to verify your identity before completing the request.
6. If the form doesn't work, email privacy@senioroi.com or call (844) 295-4726 with the same details.
