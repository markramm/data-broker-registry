1. Go to Infutor's Consumer Privacy portal (linked above) and open the "Individual Rights Request Form."
2. Submit your request, providing your name and address as prompted -- Infutor states it may ask for additional information to verify your identity, which its own published process describes as including your Social Security number.
3. Watch your email for Infutor's response: if they find a record, they'll send a disclosure report along with a link to activate an "Opt Out Community Site" account.
4. Use that activation link to log in, review what they have on file, and confirm you want it deleted/opted out.
5. If you'd rather not use the online form, call Infutor's toll-free privacy line at 1-866-207-1399 to start the same process by phone.
6. Because this broker's verification flow asks for sensitive ID information (SSN), only proceed if you're comfortable sharing that, and consider whether you want to redact/limit what you send in writing.
