1. Find your device's advertising ID. On iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising. On Android: Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
2. Email privacy@onaudience.com. Say you are a California resident opting out of the sale of your personal information, and include the advertising ID(s) from step 1.
3. Alternatively, use the forms at https://onaudience.com/opt-out/: one opts out by email address, one sets an opt-out on OnAudience's third-party cookie in the browser you're using, and one takes a mobile device ID. These forms show a reCAPTCHA.
4. Cookie opt-outs only cover the browser you used. If you clear cookies, set it again.
