1. Go to https://dmsunsub.io/ (Digital Media Solutions' CCPA/privacy request form) and follow the prompts to submit a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" request, solving the CAPTCHA if prompted.
2. Alternatively, email consumerinquiries@dmsgroup.com with your full name and the email address(es) associated with any DMS-affiliated offers, and state that you are requesting to opt out of the sale/sharing of your personal information under CCPA.
3. You can also call (800) 290-4010 or mail a written request to Digital Media Solutions, LLC, 4800 140th Avenue North, Suite 101, Clearwater, FL 33762.
4. DMS states it acts on opt-out requests within fifteen (15) business days.
5. Note that opting out of sale/sharing does not necessarily stop all direct marketing from DMS itself -- you may need to separately unsubscribe from individual campaigns.
