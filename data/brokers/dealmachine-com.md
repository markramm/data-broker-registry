1. Go to https://privacyrequest.dealmachine.com/remove (DealMachine's "Request to Remove Your Information" form). This is for people listed in DealMachine's property data, not for DealMachine customers closing an account.
2. Under "What would you like to remove?", pick "My profile". To remove only one detail, pick "My email", "My phone" or "Unlink me from a property".
3. Enter your first name, last name and email address.
4. Phone number is optional, but it helps DealMachine find your record. If you enter one, tick the box agreeing to receive a one-time verification code by text or automated call, and enter the code when it arrives.
5. Use "Additional Information" for anything that helps match your record, such as a property address you own or owned. Then submit.
6. Watch for an email saying your request is being processed. If you are a judge or another covered person under New Jersey's Daniel's Law or a similar law, email privacy@dealmachine.com instead for expedited handling.
