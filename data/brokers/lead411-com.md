1. Go to https://www.lead411.com/your-privacy-choices/.
2. Select "Opt-out of personal data sales" (or the closest matching privacy-rights option) from the list of request types.
3. Choose your country and state from the dropdowns.
4. Enter your email address so Lead411 can send a verification code.
5. Agree to the Terms of Service checkbox and submit the form.
6. Check your email for the verification code, enter it to confirm your identity, and complete the request.
7. If you don't receive a response or want an alternative channel, email support@lead411.io directly, or call +1.877.673.1022, describing the record you want removed.
