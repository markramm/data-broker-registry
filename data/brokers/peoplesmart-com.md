1. Go to peoplesmart.com, scroll to the footer, and click "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" (this routes to BeenVerified's opt-out portal at beenverified.com/app/optout/search).
2. Enter your first and last name and select your state, then click "Search."
3. Locate the record that matches you and click "Proceed to Opt Out" next to it.
4. Enter an email address you use for opt-out requests, complete the CAPTCHA, and click "Send Verification Email."
5. Check your inbox for a message from BeenVerified and click "Verify Opt Out" to finalize the request.
6. If more than one listing matches your name, repeat the process for each one -- opting out one record does not automatically clear duplicates.
