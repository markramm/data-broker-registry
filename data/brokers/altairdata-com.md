1. Go to Altair's request portal at https://datacloudhome.atlassian.net/servicedesk/customer/portal/12 (linked from https://altairdata.com/consumer-info/).
2. Choose "Individual: Opt-Out/Deletion Request Form".
3. Enter the email address where you want the confirmation, your full name, first and last name, and your street address, city, state and ZIP.
4. Under "Opt-Out/Deletion Preferences", tick the options you want. To be removed entirely, tick "Deletion of My Consumer Information" along with the opt-outs of sale and sharing.
5. Tick "I Understand" to confirm you are submitting for yourself, then click "Send". The page may run an invisible CAPTCHA check.
6. Watch for the confirmation email. Altair says it processes requests within 45 days.
7. If you would rather not use the portal, mail the same request with your full name and address to Altair Data Resources, 1749 Mallory Lane, Ste 201, Brentwood, TN 37027. You can call 1-800-241-6631 with questions.
