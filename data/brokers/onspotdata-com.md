1. If you can, find your phone's advertising ID. On Android it is under Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads). iPhone doesn't show it in Settings, so skip this step there. The ID helps OnSpot match its device-based location records to you.
2. Email optout@onspotdata.com with the subject "Privacy Opt-Out Request".
3. In the body, ask them to stop selling or sharing your personal information and to delete the location and device data they hold about you under the CCPA/Delete Act. Include your name, email and, if you have it, your mobile advertising ID.
4. Separately, reset or delete your phone's advertising ID and turn off app tracking in your device settings, so new location data is harder to link to you.
5. Keep a copy of the email and follow up at privacy@onspotdata.com if you get no reply within 45 days.
