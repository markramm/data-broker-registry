1. Go to Spectrum's privacy statement page at https://spectrummailinglists.com/privacy-statement/ and find the "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" section.
2. Follow the linked Google Form for opt-out requests.
3. Provide your full legal name and complete mailing address -- the company states it cannot process requests without both.
4. If you've received mail under more than one name variation (e.g. a maiden name, a nickname, or a slightly different spelling), submit a separate request for each variation.
5. Alternatively, email lori@spectrumlists.com or call 800.227.6245 and ask to be placed on Spectrum's suppression file and opted out of data sale/sharing.
6. Because Spectrum aggregates and resells lists sourced from many upstream providers, expect your information to reappear from other sources over time even after a successful opt-out here.
