1. If you have a myFICO/FICO account, log in and go to the FICO Trust Center's "Manage your Privacy Preferences" section to opt out of marketing/data-sharing directly.
2. If you don't have an account, or the Trust Center doesn't cover your request, email privacyteam@fico.com (FICO's Data Protection Officer) and ask to opt out of the sale/sharing of your personal information and/or to delete your data.
3. Alternatively, call (858) 369-8101 to reach the same privacy team by phone.
4. For marketing emails specifically, you can also just click "unsubscribe" in the footer of any FICO marketing message for a faster, narrower opt-out of that channel.
5. FICO states it verifies your identity before acting on privacy requests, so be ready to confirm basic identifying details (name, email on file) if asked.
