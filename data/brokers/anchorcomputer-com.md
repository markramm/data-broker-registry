1. Go to the Anchor Computer opt-out form at https://ecom2.anchorcomputer.com/privacyrequest.
2. Select the request type for opting out of sale/sharing of your personal information (California/CPRA rights).
3. Enter your first and last name, mailing address, phone number, and email address.
4. Enter your date of birth and the last 4 digits of your Social Security number, which Anchor Computer uses to match and verify your record in their marketing database.
5. Submit the form.
6. Alternatively, skip the form and email the same information directly to privacyteam@anchorcomputer.com, or call 1-800-646-2563 to request opt-out/deletion by phone.

**Sensitive-data note:** this process asks for the last 4 digits of your SSN and your date of birth. Only proceed if you're comfortable sharing that; the phone and email channels ask for the same information, so there's no lower-friction path available.
