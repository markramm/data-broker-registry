1. Go to the [Criminal Pages opt-out form](https://members.criminalpages.com/customer/opt-out). Note: this page currently presents an SSL certificate for a different domain (infopay.com), so your browser will likely show a security warning -- proceed only if you're comfortable verifying the site yourself, or use the alternatives below instead.
2. On the form, enter your full name, email, and address, and submit the removal request.
3. If you'd rather avoid the broken-certificate warning, opt out via Criminal Pages' parent company InfoTracer's shared portal at [infotracer.com/optout](https://infotracer.com/optout/), which removes your data from Criminal Pages as well.
4. As a mail/fax alternative, send a written removal request to: Data Management Department, Criminal Pages, PO Box 990142, Boston, MA 02199, or fax it to 617-507-0410.
5. You can also call 800-309-9351 with questions about your request's status.
