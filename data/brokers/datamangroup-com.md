1. Go to Dataman Group's [Privacy Request Form](https://www.datamangroup.com/privacy-request-form/).
2. Fill in your first and last name, state, email, and your full mailing address (street, city, state, postal code). Dataman's data is mailing-list data, so use the address where you receive marketing mail. Phone is optional.
3. Answer "Yes" to "I am human", tick the certification box, complete the CAPTCHA if one appears, and submit.
4. The form has no request-type choice. If Dataman's acknowledgment email asks what you want, reply that you want your information deleted, suppressed from future list licensing, and not sold or shared.
5. If the form doesn't work, email privacy@datamangroup.com with the same details, or mail Dataman Group Inc., PO Box 970123, Boca Raton, FL 33497-0123.
6. California residents can also cover Dataman, along with every other registered broker, through the state's free DROP tool.
