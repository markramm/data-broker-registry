1. Note that peopleverified.com is not a standalone site -- it permanently redirects to peoplesmart.com, which is operated by BeenVerified.
2. Go to the [BeenVerified opt-out search page](https://www.beenverified.com/app/optout/search) directly (the same portal peoplesmart.com's own "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" footer link routes to).
3. Enter your first and last name and select your state, then click "Search."
4. Locate the record that matches you and click "Proceed to Opt Out" next to it.
5. Enter an email address you use for opt-out requests, complete the CAPTCHA, and click "Send Verification Email."
6. Check your inbox for a message from BeenVerified and click "Verify Opt Out" to finalize the request.
7. If more than one listing matches your name, repeat the process for each one -- see this repo's peoplesmart-com.md and beenverified-com.md entries, since opting out here also covers those brands.
