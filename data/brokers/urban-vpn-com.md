1. If you use any Urban VPN product (VPN app or browser extension), open its settings and switch off the browsing-data sharing toggle, and use the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" option there or in the footer of urban-vpn.com.
2. Turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser if it supports it; Urban says it treats a GPC signal as an opt-out for that browser or device.
3. To have your data deleted or to opt out of sale more broadly, email privacy@urban-vpn.com saying you are exercising your right to opt out of the sale/sharing of your personal information (and, if you want, to delete it).
4. If you have the browser extension installed, include your Panelist ID: right-click a page, choose Inspect, open the Application (or Storage) tab, expand the extension's local storage, and copy the value of the key "panalyticsid". Urban only keeps that link for about 90 days, so do this promptly.
5. Answer any identity-verification follow-up; Urban says it may deny a request it cannot verify and will explain how to appeal.
6. Uninstalling the products stops future collection; the opt-out request covers what was already collected.
