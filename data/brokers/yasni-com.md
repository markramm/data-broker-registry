1. Search your name on yasni.com and note the URLs of any Yasni-hosted profile pages ("Exposé") or search-result entries that show your information.
2. Email datenschutz@yasni.de (Yasni's data-protection contact) and state that you are exercising your GDPR right to erasure/objection, listing the profile URL(s) you found and your full name.
3. If you specifically created or claimed a self-authored Exposé/account on Yasni and want it deleted, you can instead (or additionally) email info@yasni.de with a short deletion request -- Yasni states it will confirm by email once your account/Exposé is deleted.
4. For search results that summarize content originally published on a different website (not a Yasni-hosted profile), Yasni's own guidance is that removal must be requested from that original source site, since Yasni does not control or store that content itself -- only re-indexes it.
5. Keep the confirmation email as proof of your request; Yasni does not publish a stated processing timeline, so follow up if you don't hear back within a few weeks.
