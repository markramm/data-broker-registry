1. Go to https://www.completemailinglists.com/consumer-privacy-request.
2. Choose "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" (and/or the deletion option, if separately offered).
3. Enter your full name, mailing address, and an email address so Complete Mailing Lists can match your record against their licensed-professional and consumer databases.
4. Submit the request and watch for a confirmation email.
5. Complete Mailing Lists must honor the request within the CCPA-mandated timeframe (typically within 45 days); if the online form is unavailable, use the general contact listed on their Privacy Policy page instead.
