1. Go to the [FastPeopleSearch opt-out page](https://www.fastpeoplesearch.com/optout).
2. Enter your email address, check the authorization box, and solve the CAPTCHA -- expect to be asked to solve more than one CAPTCHA during this process, the site is aggressive about bot checks.
3. Search for your listing using your full name, city, and state.
4. Open your specific profile page and select the option to remove your record.
5. Solve the final CAPTCHA and submit the removal request.
6. Check your email within 24 hours and click the confirmation link -- if you miss the window, you'll need to restart the whole process. FastPeopleSearch says removal typically completes within about three days.
