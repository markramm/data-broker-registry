1. Go to https://privacy.5x5data.com/5x5/opt-out.
2. Enter your email address, first name and last name, and pick your country of residence. Leave the authorized-agent email blank if you're filing for yourself.
3. Under "Request," choose "Request to delete/erase my personal information." Submit a second request with "Do not sell or share my personal information" if you also want the sale opt-out recorded separately.
4. Complete the Cloudflare check and submit.
5. 5x5 may email you to confirm you own the address. Reply or click the link in that email.
6. If the form won't load, email privacy@5x5coop.com with the same details and request, or call 1-512-827-9312.
