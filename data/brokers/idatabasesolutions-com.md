1. Go to https://idatabasesolutions.com/personal-information-request-all-other-states/ ("Personal Information Request"). The same form is used for California residents.
2. Enter your first and last name, email, and the street address, city, state and postal code your mail arrives at. IDS uses these to find your record.
3. Tick "I request that any information about me will not be sold to anyone and that you permanently delete any information about me." Tick the "view" box too if you also want a copy of what they hold.
4. Submit the form. IDS says a matching record is deleted within 30 days.
5. If you've lived at several addresses, submit once per address. IDS may contact you to confirm your identity before acting.
6. You can also call (888) 948-0764 to make the request by phone.
