1. Go to the "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" page at https://sourceitmarketing.com/privacy/.
2. Use the "Check/Manage/Delete your Data Record" tool: enter your email address to check whether SOURCEit has a record on you.
3. If a record is found, you'll receive an email with a link to view and remove your data -- follow that link to complete the deletion.
4. If you'd rather not use the instant-check tool, use the manual request form instead: enter your contact email, the email address(es) you want investigated or removed, select "Opt out of sale or sharing of personal information" from the subject dropdown, check the California-residency box if applicable, and add any details in the comments field.
5. Submit the manual request and allow time for SOURCEit to process it, since the page notes this path is a manual review rather than an instant removal.
