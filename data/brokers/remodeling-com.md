2. Enter your first and last name, email address, and phone number.
3. Enter your street address, state of residence (California residents only, per the form), and ZIP code.
4. Check the box(es) for "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" and/or "Delete My Personal Information" as desired.

6. If you are outside California or the form does not apply to you, email privacy@remodeling.com directly with your opt-out/deletion request.
