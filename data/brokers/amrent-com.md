1. AmRent does not offer a self-service "opt out of sale" -- as an FCRA-regulated tenant-screening company, it is exempt from that kind of request, and if you submit one anyway it may be denied.
2. If you were denied housing or want to see what's in your AmRent tenant-screening report, request your free copy by mail or fax: download the "Request My Report Form" from [amrent.com/privacy](http://www.amrent.com/privacy) or [amrent.com/consumer_assistance](http://www.amrent.com/consumer_assistance), fill it out, and mail it to AmRent Consumer Assistance, PO Box 530091, Atlanta, GA 30353 (or call to ask about faxing it).
3. To dispute inaccurate information on your report, use the "Consumer Dispute Form" from the same pages and submit it the same way (mail/fax); AmRent will investigate and correct or remove disputed items.
4. You can also call AmRent Consumer Assistance at 888-898-6196 with questions about a report or dispute.
5. If a landlord or property manager denied your application based on an AmRent report, that decision was made by them, not AmRent -- follow up with the landlord directly, since AmRent can't approve or deny applications itself.
6. Email (consumers@amrent.com) exists but AmRent's own materials discourage using it for sensitive requests -- prefer phone or mail.
