1. Email privacy@brandwatch.com and state that you are submitting a CCPA opt-out-of-sale and/or deletion request for your personal data (name, social media handle, and any public social content Brandwatch has collected about you).
2. Include your full name and the social media handle(s) or profile URL(s) you want removed, so Brandwatch's team can locate the matching author records.
3. Alternatively, submit the same request through Brandwatch's web-based Data Subject Access Request form at https://www.brandwatch.com/p/legal-data-subject-access-request/, or call 1-855-368-1500.
4. Brandwatch states it will act on verified requests under CCPA; expect a confirmation reply to your email address.
5. Because Brandwatch collects only publicly posted social media content, also consider tightening the privacy settings on your social media accounts (e.g. making profiles private) to reduce what can be re-collected in the future.
