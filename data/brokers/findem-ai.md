1. Go to https://www.findem.ai/privacy-rights.
2. Enter your first and last name and the email address Findem is likely to hold for you, such as a work or professional email.
3. For request type, choose "Data deletion" (or a combination if you also want a copy of your data).
4. In the comments box, list each request you are making, for example "Delete my profile from the Talent Data Cloud and stop selling or sharing my personal information." Add any other emails or profile URLs that would help them find you.
5. Submit the form. If it doesn't work, email the same request to privacy@findem.ai.
