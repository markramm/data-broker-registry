1. Go to the [6sense Privacy Request Center](https://privacy.6sense.com/).
2. Select your country (and state, if in the US), then click "Start Deletion Request."
3. Enter your first name, last name and email. Under "Data subject's relationship with 6sense," pick the option that fits (for most people, that you're not a customer).
4. Optionally note in the comments that you also want any sale or sharing of your data to stop, citing your state privacy rights.
5. Click "Review Request," confirm and submit, then watch for a confirmation email and follow any link in it.
6. If the form fails, email privacy@6sense.com with the same request.
