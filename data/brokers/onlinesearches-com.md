1. Search for your own name on publicrecords.onlinesearches.com (or intelius.com, which powers this site's data) to find your listing, and copy its exact URL.
2. Go to the Intelius help center (intelius.zendesk.com/hc/en-us) and search for "opt out" or "remove my information" to find the current dedicated opt-out article -- the linked URL is a general help-center homepage, not a direct opt-out form, so you'll need to navigate from there.
3. Follow the article's link to the opt-out form and paste in your listing URL and full name as requested.
4. Complete the CAPTCHA challenge if prompted.
5. If the web form doesn't resolve your removal, call (206) 301-5900 and request removal by phone, referencing the listing URL you found in step 1.
6. Because this is a people-search site, check back periodically since removed listings can reappear after data refreshes.
