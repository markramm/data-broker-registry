1. Email privacychoices@cc.pch.com and state that you are exercising your CCPA right to opt out of the sale/sharing of your personal information; include your full name and the email address associated with your PCH account or entries.
2. Alternatively, go to any page on pch.com, scroll to the footer, and click "Your Privacy Choices" to submit the opt-out through PCH's self-service tool instead of email.
3. If you'd rather mail the request, send it to Consumer & Privacy Affairs, PCH Digital, LLC, 2121 Biscayne Blvd #1365, Miami, FL 33137.
4. Note that opting out of sale/sharing is separate from unsubscribing from PCH's own marketing emails or closing a sweepstakes account -- if you also want to stop receiving PCH mailings/emails directly, use the unsubscribe link in those communications or your account settings in addition to this opt-out.
