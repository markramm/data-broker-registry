1. Go to https://data-removal.realeflow.com/submit, Realeflow's Privacy Rights Request form (linked as "Do Not Sell My Information" in the site footer).
2. Under "What would you like to do?", choose "Do Not Sell / Share My Personal Information". For deletion, submit a second request choosing "Delete My Data".
3. Enter your first and last name, street address, city, state, ZIP code and a 10-digit phone number. All are required. Use the property address Realeflow is most likely to have for you.
4. Email is optional for an opt-out, but adding it gets you a confirmation.
5. Complete the CAPTCHA and submit. Opt-outs don't need identity verification; deletions are handled within 45 days.
6. For problems, email privacy@realeflow.com or call 1 (855) 545-2095.
