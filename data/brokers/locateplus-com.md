1. Email customerservice@locateplus.com and state that you are a California resident exercising your CCPA rights to opt out of the sale/sharing of your personal information and/or request deletion.
2. Include your full name and any other identifying details (past addresses, phone numbers) that will help LocatePLUS locate your record among its aggregated public/private-records database.
3. Alternatively, call 1-888-746-3463 to make the same request by phone.
4. LocatePLUS states it does not sell or share information collected directly through its own website, but it aggregates and resells third-party public/private records to subscribers (investigators, collections agencies, etc.) -- ask explicitly that your record be suppressed from search results and reports, not just from marketing.
5. Because this is a B2B investigative-records service rather than a self-service consumer opt-out form, expect a manual back-and-forth rather than an instant confirmation; follow up if you don't hear back within a few weeks.
