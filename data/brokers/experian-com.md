1. Go to https://www.optoutprescreen.com (the official Consumer Credit Reporting Industry site that processes opt-outs for Experian, Equifax, TransUnion, and Innovis together), or call 1-888-567-8688.
2. Choose whether you want to opt out for 5 years or permanently.
3. If opting out online for 5 years, enter your name, address, and date of birth as prompted and submit -- no CAPTCHA or ID upload required.
4. If you choose the permanent option, you'll be mailed a "Notice of Election to Opt-Out Permanently" form that you must sign and return by mail to complete the permanent opt-out.
5. Your request becomes effective with Experian within about 5 business days, though it can take a few months before you notice fewer preapproved offers arriving.
