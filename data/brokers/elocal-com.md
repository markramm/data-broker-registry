1. Go to https://www.elocal.com/ccpa/opt_outs/new (linked from "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" on eLocal's California Privacy Notice).
2. Enter your first and last name, street address, city, state, zip code and email. Use the details you gave when you requested a local service through eLocal or one of its partner sites.
3. Tick "Do not sell my information to third parties" and submit. eLocal says it acts on opt-outs within 15 days.
4. If you also want eLocal to delete what it holds, submit a second request at https://www.elocal.com/ccpa/privacy_rights_requests/new.
5. If the form doesn't work, call 833-854-1932 or email correspondence@elocal.com asking eLocal to stop selling or sharing your personal information.
