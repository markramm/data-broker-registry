1. Go to https://go.kindsight.io/privacy-opt-out ("Privacy Opt-Out Request").
2. Enter your first and last name, a real (not disposable) email address, your mailing address, country, city and postal code.
3. Submit the form. It runs an invisible CAPTCHA check.
4. To find out which third-party data providers supplied Kindsight's information about you, or to have your charitable-gift records (VeriGift) searched and removed, use Kindsight's privacy webform at https://privacyportal.onetrust.com/webform/aaccdd68-ac9c-49cc-a5fa-7ab75cd7892c/204bc335-98a1-412e-9a92-3dfd8b86e15f. Then opt out with each provider it names.
5. For anything the forms don't cover, email privacy@kindsight.io or call 1-800-655-7729.
