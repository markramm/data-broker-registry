1. Go to the [Yobi "Do not sell or share my personal information" form](https://www.yobi.ai/opt-out).
2. Fill in your first and last name, email, phone number, street address, city, state, ZIP, country and date of birth. Yobi says it uses these only to find your records and verify the request.
3. Under "I am submitting on behalf of", choose **Myself** (or **My household**).
4. Under "Select the right(s) you want to exercise", tick **Do Not Sell My Information**, and also **Delete My Information** if you want your data removed.
5. Submit the request and watch your email for any verification message from Yobi.
