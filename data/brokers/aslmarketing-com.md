1. Go to the Deep Sync consumer opt-out portal at [privacy.deepsync.com](https://privacy.deepsync.com/) (ASL Marketing operates under the Deep Sync corporate umbrella; there is no longer a separate ASL-specific opt-out form).
2. Enter your full name, mailing address, and email address, and select "Do not sell/share my personal information" (and "Please delete my personal information" if you also want deletion).
3. Complete the CAPTCHA and submit the request.
4. Alternatively, email privacy.compliance@deepsync.com directly with the name and address you want removed -- note this email-only path does not send a confirmation, so the form is the more verifiable route.
5. Because ASL Marketing/Deep Sync compiles from public records and licensed lists, expect the possibility of re-addition and re-check every few months.
