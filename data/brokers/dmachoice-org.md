1. Go to https://www.dmachoice.org/dma/member/regist.action.
2. Register an account: create a login with your email address and a password.
3. Enter your name (plus any name variations/nicknames you want covered) and your mailing address(es) -- you can list up to four additional names and addresses.
4. Choose "I do not want to receive commercial mail from companies I don't do business with" for each mail category you want to suppress.
5. Pay the $8 online administrative fee (credit card or PayPal) to complete registration -- or mail a $9 check/money order to DMAchoice, Consumer Preferences, P.O. Box 900, Cos Cob, CT 06807 if you prefer not to pay online.
6. Your preferences last 10 years; note this only reduces mail from DMA-member companies you have no existing relationship with, it won't stop all mail.
