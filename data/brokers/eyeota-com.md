1. Go to https://www.eyeota.com/data-subject-request-form and fill in the data subject request form with your name and email address, selecting "opt-out" or "delete my data" as the request type.
2. Alternatively, email privacy@eyeota.com directly and state that you want to opt out of Eyeota's collection/sale of your data and/or have your data deleted.
3. As another option, call the industry opt-out line at 1-866-467-8688 (1-866-I-OPT-OUT) and provide Eyeota's service code: 714.
4. Eyeota states it will acknowledge your request within about 72 hours and fully process it within 30 days.
5. Because Eyeota supplies data into many downstream advertising platforms, consider also using a broader industry opt-out tool (e.g. the NAI or DAA opt-out pages) to cover other data partners at the same time.
