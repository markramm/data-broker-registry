1. Go to https://privacychoice.com/ (Semcasting's designated opt-out portal) and submit a request to opt out of the sale of your personal information.
2. Alternatively, call the toll-free number 888-401-0387 to request the same opt-out.
3. Or email privacychoice@semcasting.com directly, stating you want to opt out of the sale/sharing of your personal information and (if desired) request deletion of any profile Semcasting holds on you.
4. Semcasting's policy states it will comply within 15 business days.
5. Note that Semcasting distinguishes between data it receives from its business clients (which it says it does not control) and data it compiles independently -- your request may need to be repeated with individual client companies if the data originated from them.
