1. Go to autoweb.com and click "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" in the footer. It opens AutoWeb's privacy request form, hosted by OneTrust.
2. Under "I am a (an)", choose Consumer.
3. Under request types, tick "Request to Opt-Out (Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information)". Also tick "Request to Delete Personal Information" if you want your records erased.
4. Enter your country of residence and state, and fill in any contact fields the form shows after your selections. Use the details you gave when you requested car quotes.
5. Complete the reCAPTCHA and submit. AutoWeb says it may contact you to verify your identity before completing the request.
6. If the form doesn't work, email AutoLegal@autoweb.com or call (800) 267-2015 ext. 3050 asking AutoWeb to stop selling or sharing your personal information.
