1. Go to https://reklaimyou.com/do-not-sell (Reklaim's "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" page). You don't need an account.
2. Enter your first name, last name and email. Phone is optional.
3. Click "OPT-OUT". Reklaim says it handles requests within 15 business days and may contact you to verify your identity.
4. To ask for deletion as well, or if the form doesn't work, email privacy@reklaimyou.com asking them to delete your personal information and stop selling or sharing it, including data they got from other data brokers.
5. If you use the Reklaim app, note that opting out ends your eligibility for its rewards; account holders can also delete their account from the site footer's "Delete my account" link.
