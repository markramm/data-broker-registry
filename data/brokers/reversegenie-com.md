1. Go to `https://infotracer.com/optout/` -- Reverse Genie is an InfoTracer-network site and removal is handled centrally through InfoTracer, not on reversegenie.com itself.
2. Enter your first and last name, select your state, enter your city, and submit.
3. Check the "I'm not a robot" verification box to see search results.
4. Find your record, check the box to select it, then click the "Remove Data" button that appears.
5. Enter your email address, optionally note a reason for opting out, complete the CAPTCHA, and submit.
6. Check your email for InfoTracer's confirmation message and click "Confirm Email" to finalize the removal.
