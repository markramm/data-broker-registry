1. Go to https://www.fhamortgagefinder.com/CCPArequest.php (also linked from the site footer as "Do Not Sell My Personal Information").
2. Choose the request type: access your information, delete your information, or opt out of the sale of your information.
3. Fill in your first and last name, email address, phone number, street address, city, state, ZIP code, and year of birth, then certify that you are the person named (or an authorized agent) and submit.
4. FHA Mortgage Finder states it aims to respond within about 10 days and has up to 45 days by law.
5. Because this site exists to pass your information to a network of third-party lenders, submitting this request should stop new sharing going forward, but it will not retroactively recall data already sent to lenders you were previously matched with.
