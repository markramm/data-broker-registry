1. Search for your address or name on neighbor.report to locate your listing; note the record's URL.
2. Open the [removal request form](https://neighbor.report/remove) -- the record URL should auto-populate if you arrived via a link on your listing page, otherwise paste it in.
3. Fill in your name, residential address, and an email address, and select the reason(s) for removal.
4. Solve the CAPTCHA and click through to the next step, then click "Remove" next to each specific piece of information on your record you want erased, and click "Apply."
5. Expect processing to take roughly 10-14 business days; use a disposable or secondary email since data brokers are known to add submitted addresses to marketing lists.
6. Re-check periodically in case the listing is re-added from a later data refresh.
