1. Go to https://liveramp.com/privacy/my-privacy-choices, LiveRamp's consumer opt-out submission portal.
2. Submit the web form with your first name, last name, and email address -- LiveRamp states these three fields are required for an effective opt-out.
3. Alternatively, call 844-678-0045 and enter service code 1; be ready to give your first and last name, phone number, home address, and email address. A LiveRamp representative will follow up to complete the request.
4. If you only want to stop tracking on a specific device or browser without submitting personal details, LiveRamp also offers a no-PII mobile-device opt-out and a browser-cookie opt-out, each covering only that device/browser.
5. LiveRamp states it will respond to opt-out requests within 15 days of receipt.
6. For California-specific rights language, see https://liveramp.com/privacy/california-privacy-notice/.
