1. Go to radaris.com and search your name to find your listing; results may include sponsored/ad links mixed in, so make sure you open your actual profile, not an ad, and copy its URL.
2. Go to the [Radaris privacy control page](https://radaris.com/control-privacy) (linked from "Remove My Information" at the bottom of your profile).
3. Enter your full name, email address, and the profile URL, then solve the CAPTCHA and submit.
4. Follow any additional confirmation steps the form presents -- Radaris's flow is multi-page, so don't stop until you reach a "request submitted" confirmation screen.
5. Check your email for a verification message from Radaris and click the link to finalize the removal.
6. Use a disposable or secondary email address if you're worried about being added to marketing lists, and expect the whole process (including the email wait) to take up to an hour.
