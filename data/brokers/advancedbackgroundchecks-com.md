1. Go to advancedbackgroundchecks.com, scroll to the footer, and click "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" to reach the [removal page](https://www.advancedbackgroundchecks.com/removal).
2. Enter an email address and solve the CAPTCHA, then click "Begin Removal Process."
3. Search for your record using your first and last name; city/state or zip and age can help narrow results but aren't strictly required.
4. Find your matching record, click "VIEW DETAILS," then click the "REMOVE MY RECORD" button on your profile.
5. Check your email for a confirmation message from Advanced Background Checks and click the link to finalize -- removal is reported to complete within about 72 hours.
6. If you have multiple listings under your name, repeat the process separately for each one.
