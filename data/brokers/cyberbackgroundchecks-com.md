1. First find your listing by searching your name on cyberbackgroundchecks.com so you know which record to reference.
2. Go to the [removal page](https://www.cyberbackgroundchecks.com/removal) and submit the short initial form: your name and an email address (consider using an email you don't mind putting on a marketing list), then solve the CAPTCHA and send the request.
3. Check that inbox for a verification email from CyberBackgroundChecks and click through to the second, more detailed removal form.
4. On that second form, fill in your full name, date of birth, and address, solve the CAPTCHA again, and submit.
5. Allow up to 48 hours for the listing to be removed -- both steps have to be completed, the first form alone does not finish the opt-out.
6. Because this site re-ingests public-record feeds, plan to re-check and repeat the process every few months if your listing reappears.
