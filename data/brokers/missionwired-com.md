1. Go to https://info.missionwired.com/coop-optout (MissionWired's "Sale Opt-Out Webform") in a browser with JavaScript enabled.
2. Fill in the form with the name, email address and other contact details that political or nonprofit groups may have for you (use every email address you have given to campaigns or charities), and submit it.
3. If you also want MissionWired to delete what it holds, not only stop selling it, submit a request at https://info.missionwired.com/personal-information-request or email info@missionwired.com asking for deletion.
4. If you visit missionwired.com, also open "Your Privacy Choices" in the site footer and click "Reject Optional" (or turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser) to opt out of cookie-based sharing. MissionWired says you need both this and the webform to opt out completely.
5. If MissionWired denies your request, you can appeal by emailing info@missionwired.com.
