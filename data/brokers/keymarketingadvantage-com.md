1. Go to KMA's [Do Not Sell or Share form](https://keymarketingadvantage.com/do_not_sell_or_share_my_personal_information).
2. Fill in every required field: first and last name, email, street address, city, state and ZIP. KMA matches people by name and postal address, so use the address where you receive marketing mail. If you have moved recently, send a second request for your previous address.
3. Tick both "Delete My Data" and "Do Not Sell or Share My Data". "Do Not Use My Data" is also available.
4. The message box is required. A short line such as "Please delete my record and stop selling or sharing my information" is enough.
5. Complete the CAPTCHA and submit. KMA says it responds within 45 days.
6. If the form doesn't work, email privacy@keymarketingadvantage.com or call 203-491-2200 with your full name and full mailing address. You can also mail a request to Key Marketing Advantage, Compliance Officer, 7 Edmond Road, Suite 100, Newtown, CT 06470.
