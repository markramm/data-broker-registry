1. Go to https://donorbureau.com/opt-out (the "Opt-Out" link in DonorBureau's site footer). The removal form loads in a frame on that page.
2. Enter your first and last name. Then add each contact detail you want removed from DonorBureau's files: email address, cellphone, and mailing address (street, city, state, zip). None of these is required, but DonorBureau only removes what it can match.
3. The form also asks for a birthday. It's optional, so you can leave it blank.
4. Submit the form. Submit it again for any other email address, phone number or past address you want removed.
5. If the form doesn't work, email CCPA@donorbureau.com asking DonorBureau to remove your contact information and stop selling your personal information. List the names, emails, phone numbers and addresses to suppress.
