1. Gunderson Direct operates under the domain gundir.com; gundersondirect.com redirects there automatically.
2. For a general privacy/marketing-preference request, email contactus@gundir.com and ask to be removed from any mailing lists sourced from or maintained by Gunderson Direct/Gundir, citing your CCPA right to opt out of sale/sharing.
3. Include your full name, mailing address, and (if you have received mail attributable to them) any reference number or client name printed on the mail piece.
4. Separately, Gunderson Direct's own opt-out page (gundir.com/opt-out/) does not process removals directly -- it points to the national do-not-mail registry. Registering there (dmachoice.org or the FTC's guidance) will reduce mail from many list-based marketers, including this one, but is not Gunderson-specific.
5. Because this is a B2B list/marketing agency rather than a consumer-facing opt-out portal, expect no confirmation page or tracking number; keep a copy of your email for your records.
