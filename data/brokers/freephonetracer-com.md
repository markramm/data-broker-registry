1. Go to BeenVerified's opt-out page (linked above), since FreePhoneTracer shares BeenVerified's underlying database and has no separate opt-out form of its own.
2. Search for your listing by entering your first and last name and selecting your state, then click Search.
3. Find the record that matches you and click "Proceed to Opt Out."
4. Enter your email address and complete the CAPTCHA challenge, then submit the form.
5. Check your inbox for a verification email from BeenVerified and click "Verify Opt-Out."
6. Your listing should be removed within 1-3 days. If multiple records match your name, email optout@beenverified.com directly to request removal of the others.
