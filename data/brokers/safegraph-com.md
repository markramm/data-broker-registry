1. Email privacy@safegraph.com and state that you are exercising your CCPA/CPRA rights to know, delete, and/or opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information (including any location/mobility data that may be associated with you).
2. Include your full name and any identifying details (such as a mobile advertising ID, if you know it, or the approximate time period/geography of your mobile device use) that could help SafeGraph locate records associated with you -- SafeGraph does not offer a self-service search tool, so specificity helps.
3. If you prefer not to email, you can send a written request to SafeGraph's Office of General Counsel at 1580 N Logan St, Ste 660, #53755, Denver, CO 80203.
4. SafeGraph states its products are built from third-party-licensed and aggregated location data about places rather than direct collection from individuals, so expect them to ask clarifying questions about which underlying data source(s) may include information about you.
5. Ask specifically about opting out of any state-specific data sale/sharing or targeted-advertising uses, since SafeGraph's policy notes these rights vary by state of residence.
6. Follow up if you don't receive confirmation within the timeframe required by your state's privacy law (generally 45 days under CCPA/CPRA).
