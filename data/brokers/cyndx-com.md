1. Go to https://www.cyndx.com/California-Do-Not-Track/ and fill out the privacy request form with your name and email.
2. Provide as much identifying detail as you can (company affiliation, role) so Cyndx can locate any business-contact record associated with you.
3. If the form is unavailable or you prefer email, send your request to privacy@cyndx.com describing what you want (access, deletion, or opt-out of sale).
4. Alternatively, call (877) 237-4418 and ask for the Consumer Privacy Coordinator to submit your request by phone.
5. Cyndx's registration states it holds only business-related contact information, not consumer/personal data -- if you don't have a professional/investment-industry presence, you likely have no record to remove.
