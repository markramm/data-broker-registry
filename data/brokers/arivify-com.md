1. Go to arivify.com and browse by State, then City, to find the page listing your address or property.
2. Open the profile matching your address/name and copy its full URL.
3. Go to the removal page at https://www.arivify.com/removal.
4. Enter your full name, your email address, and paste the profile URL you copied.
5. Click "REMOVE PROFILE" to submit the request.
6. Allow anywhere from 24 hours to several weeks for removal, and expect to re-check afterward -- user reports indicate this opt-out form is not always reliable and data can reappear or the form may fail to find your listing.
7. If the removal page doesn't recognize your address, try contacting Arivify directly via the email listed on their site referencing your profile URL.
