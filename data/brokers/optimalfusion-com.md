1. Go to https://optimalfusion.com/do-not-sell-my-personal-information/.
2. Choose "Do not sell or share my information" (or "Delete my information" if you want deletion as well).
3. Enter your first name, last name, postal code, and email address.
4. Agree to the sworn declaration confirming the information you supplied is true and correct, then submit the form.
5. Alternatively, email dataprotectionofficer@optimalfusion.com or call 866-764-1025 to make the same request.
6. Optimal Fusion states it cannot complete the request if you fail to verify your identity, so respond promptly to any identity-confirmation follow-up.
7. Keep a record of your submission in case you need to follow up.
