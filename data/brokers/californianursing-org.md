1. Go to https://www.californianursing.org/ and search for your own name to find your license listing.
2. Open your listing and note the record ID shown in the bottom-right corner of the license-details block.
3. Email removals@licensedata.org with the record ID and your full name as shown on the listing, requesting removal from californianursing.org.
4. The site explicitly discourages delisting, warning it may make it harder for others to verify your license through this particular unofficial site -- your official license status remains independently verifiable through the California Board of Registered Nursing.
5. This site is part of a network run by the same operator (californiabrokers.org, californiaengineering.org, californialicensing.org, californiapharmacists.org, and others linked in the footer) -- if you hold other licenses, check the related sites for a matching listing and repeat the removal request there.
