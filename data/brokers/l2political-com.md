1. Go to https://l2-data.com/optout1-667457/ (the L2 Political opt-out form, now hosted under L2's rebranded l2-data.com site).
2. Enter your first and last name and your full mailing address (street, city, state, ZIP) so L2 can locate your voter/consumer record.
3. Optionally add a phone number or email address.
4. Solve the simple math CAPTCHA (e.g. "14 + 3 =") and check the consent box confirming your identity.
5. Submit the form. L2 states it will remove your contact information "as soon as reasonably practicable."
6. California residents can also look for the "Do Not Sell My Personal Data" link on l2-data.com's footer for a CCPA-specific route, or email privacyrequests@l2political.com, support@l2-data.com, or info@l2-data.com, or call 1-800-842-5478.
7. Keep a record of your submission; re-check periodically since voter files are refreshed from public records on an ongoing basis.
