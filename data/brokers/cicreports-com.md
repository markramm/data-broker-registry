1. Go to [consumers.cicreports.com/register](https://consumers.cicreports.com/register) and create a free Consumer Portal account: first/last name, date of birth, email, address, and a password (driver's license number is optional, not required).
2. Once registered, use the portal to view what CIC has on file and submit a dispute for any item you believe is inaccurate or shouldn't be there.
3. If you'd rather not create an account, call CIC Consumer Relations at 888-316-4242 (Mon-Fri, 9am-7pm ET) to start a dispute by phone.
4. As a mail/fax alternative, write to CIC Consumer Relations, 3700 Crestwood Pkwy #300, Duluth, GA 30096 (fax 888-797-2254) -- note this path asks for more, including your SSN and a copy of a driver's license or state ID, so prefer the online portal or phone if you can.
5. If you are a survivor of human trafficking seeking removal of adverse items tied to that, CIC has a dedicated expedited process -- call 888-316-4242 and ask for it specifically.
6. This is a dispute/correction process, not a "stop selling my data" opt-out -- CIC operates as an FCRA consumer reporting agency and doesn't offer blanket removal.
