1. Go to https://www.data-axle.com/privacy-rights-request/ -- Exact Data's opt-out/deletion requests are processed by its parent company, Data Axle.
2. Select the request type you want: opt out of sale/sharing, opt out of behavioral-advertising data use, or delete your personal information.
3. Fill in your name and email address (and any other requested identifying details) and submit the form.
4. If you'd rather use email, send your request directly to privacyteam@data-axle.com, stating you want to opt out of data sale/sharing and be removed from Exact Data / Data Axle marketing records.
5. If a browser add-on that broadcasts Global Privacy Control (GPC) is available to you, enabling it may also register as a valid opt-out per Data Axle's stated policy.
6. Data Axle states average completion time is about a week; if you don't hear back, follow up by replying to any confirmation email or re-submitting the form.
