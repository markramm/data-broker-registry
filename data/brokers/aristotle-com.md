1. Go to the [Aristotle personal-information deletion page](https://aristotle.com/privacy/delete-my-personal-information/).
2. Enter your first and last name (middle name optional) and your complete address.
3. Enter your email address, date of birth, and the last 4 digits of your Social Security Number.
4. Check the box authorizing Aristotle to use this information solely to verify your identity and process the deletion.
5. Complete the CAPTCHA and submit.
6. If Aristotle needs additional identity verification, they may follow up by email or phone (800-296-2747) before completing the deletion.

**Sensitive-data note:** this form asks for the last 4 digits of your SSN. Only proceed if you are comfortable sharing that; you can also request deletion by phone at 800-296-2747 instead.
