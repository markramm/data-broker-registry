1. Go to the InfoTracer opt-out portal at https://infotracer.com/optout/ (deathrecords.org is one of several InfoTracer-branded sites that share this single removal system).
2. Search for your own listing by name and state to locate the record(s) about you.
3. Select the matching listing(s) from the results.
4. Complete the CAPTCHA challenge and submit the removal request with your email address so InfoTracer can send a confirmation link.
5. Click the confirmation link in the email InfoTracer sends you to finalize the opt-out.
6. If the listing reappears or the portal won't load, call InfoTracer support at 800-309-9352 and reference deathrecords.org specifically.
