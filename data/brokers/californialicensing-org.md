1. Go to https://www.californialicensing.org/ and search for your own name to find your license listing (the site aggregates many license types/boards, so check for all listings matching your name).
2. Open each matching listing and note the record ID shown in the bottom-right corner of the license-details block.
3. Email removals@licensedata.org with the record ID(s) and your full name as shown, requesting removal from californialicensing.org.
4. The site explicitly notes it is unaffiliated with the State of California -- delisting here has no effect on your official license status, which remains verifiable through the California Department of Consumer Affairs.
5. This site overlaps with narrower sibling sites in the same operator network (californiabrokers.org, californiaengineering.org, californianursing.org, californiapharmacists.org, and others) -- a listing may appear on more than one, so repeat the removal request wherever you find a matching entry.
