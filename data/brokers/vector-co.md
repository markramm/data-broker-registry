1. Open Vector's Privacy Request Center at https://privacy.vector.co/ (vector.co/opt-out redirects there).
2. Choose your country and state of residence, then click "Start Deletion Request".
3. Enter your first and last name and the email address you want matched (a work email if Vector is likely to hold that). Under "Data subject's relationship with Vector", pick the option that fits, for example that you are not a customer.
4. Click "Review Request", check the details, and submit. Solve a CAPTCHA or confirm by email if Vector asks you to.
5. To opt out of sale or sharing instead of deleting, use the same center. Its footer link "Do not sell or share my personal information" points there too.
