1. Go to https://skydeo.com/privacy-policy/opt-out/ and follow the CCPA opt-out process described there (submit your email address to request opt-out of sale and/or deletion).
2. Alternatively, email privacy@skydeo.com directly with your opt-out/deletion request, or use the contact form at skydeo.com/contact.
3. To specifically stop interest-based mobile advertising (separate from the data-sale opt-out), install the DAA's AppChoices app (iOS/Android/Amazon) or enable "Limit Ad Tracking"/"Opt out of Ads Personalization" in your device settings.
4. EU/Swiss residents can also use youronlinechoices.com for the equivalent EU industry opt-out.
5. Watch for confirmation from Skydeo that your request has been processed.
