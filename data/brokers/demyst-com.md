1. Go to https://demyst.com/personal-information-request-california and submit the online form to request access, deletion, or opt-out of sale/sharing of your personal information under CCPA.
2. Alternatively, email privacy@demystdata.com with the same request if you prefer not to use the web form.
3. Include your full name and email address so Demyst can locate any records associated with you in their data platform.
4. If mailing a request instead, use the physical address listed in Demyst's privacy policy contact section (New York office).
5. Demyst states it will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights; keep a record of your submission date in case you need to follow up.
