1. Note that SBFE does not collect data directly from you -- it receives small-business trade/payment data from lending institutions you (or your business) have relationships with, and shares it with commercial credit bureaus.
2. Because of this, SBFE's policy asks California residents to first contact the specific lending institution(s) with which they have a relationship to make CCPA-related requests, since those lenders control the underlying data.
3. If you still want to contact SBFE directly, email ccpa@sbfe.org (or call 216-340-0696) stating you are a California resident exercising your CCPA rights to know, opt out of sale, or request deletion.
4. Be aware SBFE's policy states it may decline requests where the information is necessary for an ongoing business relationship or contractual obligation.
5. If you are not in California, this opt-out channel may not apply -- SBFE's policy frames these rights specifically around CCPA.
