1. Go to https://forddirect.privacy.saymine.io/forddirect (FordDirect's "Your Privacy Choices" page, linked from the footer of forddirect.com) and follow the prompts to submit a CCPA opt-out of sale/sharing or a deletion request.
2. Alternatively, call FordDirect's privacy line at 1-800-392-3673 (Monday-Friday 8am-11pm ET, Saturday 8am-5pm PT) to submit the same request by phone.
3. You can also email dprivacy@forddirect.com directly with your request, including your full name and the email address associated with any Ford/Lincoln dealer interactions.
4. FordDirect states it will respond to verified, valid requests within 45 calendar days.
5. If your initial request is denied or you disagree with the outcome, dprivacy@forddirect.com is also listed as the appeals contact.
