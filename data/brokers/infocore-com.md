1. Email dpo@infocore.com and state that you are submitting a CCPA request to opt out of the sale/sharing of your personal information (or to have your data deleted).
2. Include your full name, the email address(es) associated with any marketing lists you believe include you, and enough detail for Infocore to locate your record and verify you are the person the data concerns.
3. Alternatively, call (760) 607-2500, use the contact form at infocore.com/contact/, or write to Infocore Inc., 2766 Gateway Road, Carlsbad, CA 92009.
4. Infocore states it will verify your identity/authority before acting on the request and will respond free of charge within 45 days (extendable by another 45 days with notice).
