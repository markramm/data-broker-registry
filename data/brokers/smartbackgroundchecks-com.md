1. Go to the [SmartBackgroundChecks opt-out page](https://www.smartbackgroundchecks.com/optout) (or click "Do Not Sell My Info" in the site footer).
2. Enter an email address, agree to the terms, solve the CAPTCHA, and click through to begin the removal process.
3. Search for your record using your first and last name plus city/state.
4. Open the matching record and click the removal button on your profile page.
5. Check your email for a confirmation message and click the validation link -- SmartBackgroundChecks says removal typically completes within about 72 hours.
6. This site shares data with the related broker PeopleFinders; after your SmartBackgroundChecks listing clears, check PeopleFinders separately in case a linked listing remains.
