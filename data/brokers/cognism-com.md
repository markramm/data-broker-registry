1. Go to https://cognism.privacy.saymine.io/cognism, Cognism's dedicated privacy-rights portal.
2. Select the CCPA/CPRA right you want to exercise (opt-out of sale/share, delete, correct, or know/access) and complete the request with your name and email.
3. Submit the request. Cognism states it will not process the request until it can reasonably verify your identity, so be ready to respond to any follow-up verification email.
4. You will receive a verification email after submitting -- click the confirmation link to complete the process.
5. If you'd rather not use the portal, email privacy@cognism.com directly, or call the toll-free number 1-833-829-1090.
6. If your browser or extension supports Global Privacy Control (GPC), Cognism states it will honor that signal as an opt-out as well.
