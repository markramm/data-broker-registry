1. Go to the LexisNexis consumer opt-out page and click the "submit your suppression request" link to reach the online suppression form.
2. Fill in your name and address details as prompted -- this is a basic online form, no account or payment needed.
3. If you are an identity-theft victim, decide whether to attach a copy of a police report (or other documentation verifying the identity theft) -- this unlocks a stronger suppression than the basic request.
4. If you are law enforcement/a public official whose position exposes you to a threat of death or serious bodily harm, submit a letter from your supervisor confirming that instead.
5. If you're at risk for other safety reasons, attach a court protective order, police report, or similar documentation.
6. Submit the form. Note that LexisNexis suppression only affects LexisNexis's own databases -- it does not stop other data brokers or public-records sources from continuing to collect and sell the same information, so you'll still need to opt out elsewhere separately.
