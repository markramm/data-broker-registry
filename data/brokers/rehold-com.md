1. Go to rehold.com and search using your street address rather than your name.
2. Drill down through state, then "more cities" to find your city, then scan the property list for your address and click "view details."
3. On your property's page, click "information control," then click "remove" next to each piece of information you want taken down, and click "apply." You'll need to enter your name and email and solve a CAPTCHA as part of this step.
4. Check your email for a confirmation message from Rehold and click the link to finalize the removal.
5. Records should disappear within about 48 hours; if they don't, email support@rehold.com directly and describe which listing you need removed.
6. Rehold pulls from property records that get refreshed periodically, so check back and repeat the process if your listing reappears.
