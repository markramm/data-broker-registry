1. Go to https://www.becausal.com/personal-information.
2. To opt this browser out of targeted advertising, move the toggle on the page to the left. Repeat this on every browser and device you use, and again after clearing cookies. Turning on Global Privacy Control in your browser also counts as an opt-out.
3. To opt out of Becausal selling or sharing your data as a data broker, fill in the form below the toggle. Pick your state of residence, then enter your first name, last name and email address.
4. Pick the statement that says you are the person named (or their authorized agent), then submit.
5. If the form doesn't work, email privacy@becausal.com and ask them not to sell or share your personal information. Include your name, email and state.
