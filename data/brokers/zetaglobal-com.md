1. Go to Zeta's [Privacy Choices page](https://zetaglobal.com/privacy-choices/) and open the privacy request form for email-linked data.
2. Choose "Email recipient or Internet user," then select "Do not sell or share my identifiable information" and "Delete identifiable information that relates to me" (you can pick both).
3. Enter your first name, last name, email address and country. Leave the details box free of personal data, as the form asks.
4. Solve the CAPTCHA and submit, then click the verification link Zeta emails you.
5. Zeta keys its records on email address, so repeat steps 2–4 for each email address you use.
6. Back on the Privacy Choices page, also use the online behavioral advertising link (zetaglobal.com/rights-request/) in each browser you use. That covers Zeta's cookie-based data, which the email form does not.
7. California residents can instead call 1.866.709.0840. Turning on Global Privacy Control in your browser also counts as an opt-out of sale/sharing.
