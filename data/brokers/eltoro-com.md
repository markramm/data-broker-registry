1. Go to https://eltoro.com/do-not-sell-my-personal-information/.
2. Complete the embedded "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information / Opt-Out Request Form" (a OneTrust privacy-request form), providing your name and email address, and any other fields it asks for.
3. Submit the form. Per El Toro's Privacy Rights Addendum, this single request opts you out of the sale/sharing of your personal information across all El Toro products and services, and also opts you out of "profiling" and "targeted advertising" use where applicable under your state's law.
4. If you'd rather not use the web form, call El Toro toll-free at 855-935-8676 and make the same request by phone.
5. For a broader cut of cross-app/cross-platform ad tracking beyond El Toro specifically, also visit the opt-out pages offered by the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) and Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA), both linked from El Toro's privacy-choices page.
6. If you separately want to know what categories of data El Toro holds about you, or to request access to or deletion of specific personal information, use the linked Access Request or Access/Deletion Request web forms on the same privacy-choices page -- these are distinct from the opt-out-of-sale request above.
