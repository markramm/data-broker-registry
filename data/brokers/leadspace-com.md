1. Go to https://www.leadspace.com/do-not-sell-form.
2. Enter your first name, last name and email address. Company and address are optional, but adding the company you work for helps Leadspace find your professional record.
3. Under "Relationship to Leadspace", choose "Other" unless one of the listed relationships applies to you.
4. Leave the free-text box empty for an opt-out of sale/sharing; use it only to name a different right (deletion, correction, access).
5. Submit the form.
6. If the form doesn't work, email info@leadspace.com citing your right to opt out of sale/sharing, or call 855-532-3772.
