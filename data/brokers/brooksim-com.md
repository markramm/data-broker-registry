1. Go to https://www.brooksim.com/privacy-form (or find "Privacy Form" in the footer of brooksim.com).
2. Fill in your full name, email address, street address, city, state, zip, country, and phone number, and select the appropriate request type (opt-out / deletion).
3. Submit the form. BrooksIM will send a confirmation email to the address you provided.
4. Open that email and click the confirmation link within 1 hour of receiving it -- the request is not processed until you confirm.
5. If you prefer not to use the form, you can instead email privacy@brooksim.com directly or call 800-531-2601 ext. 998 to submit the same request.
6. California residents can alternatively submit a single request through the state's Delete Request and Opt-Out Platform (DROP), which will also reach BrooksIM as a registered data broker.
