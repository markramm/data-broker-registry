1. Go to https://www.dnb.com and scroll to the footer, or go directly to the privacy policy at https://www.dnb.com/utility-pages/privacy-policy.html.
2. Click "Your Privacy Choices" (or "Do Not Sell My Personal Information") in the footer to open the opt-out form.
3. Enter your first and last name, company/job title, phone number, and email address; optionally add your street address, city, state, and postal code.
4. Submit the form and verify your email by clicking the confirmation link D&B sends you.
5. Expect a response/processing time of up to 15 days.
6. If your data was collected specifically through D&B's Email IQ product, you can instead call 1-800-234-3867 or email custservprivacy@dnb.com to request removal.
