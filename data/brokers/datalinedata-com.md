1. Go to https://datalinedata.com/do-not-sell-my-information/.
2. Fill in your full name, email address, and street address (city, state, ZIP).
3. Check the box(es) for the request(s) you want: view your data, delete your data, opt out of sale, opt out of behavioral advertising, and/or correct your data. Checking "opt out of sale" is the core CCPA request.
4. Submit the form. Dataline's Data Protection Officer is notified immediately and states they will respond within 10 days once your identity is verified.
5. Verify your identity via the emailed confirmation link, or call 888-990-3399 with the verification code you receive if you prefer to confirm by phone.
6. If the form is unavailable, email privacy.officer@datalinedata.com directly with the same request details.
