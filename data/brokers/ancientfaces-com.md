1. Go to ancientfaces.com and use the people-search feature (first name, last name, approximate birth year, city/state) to find the profile matching you.
2. Open the matching profile and copy its full URL.
3. Go to the AncientFaces contact form at https://www.ancientfaces.com/contact.
4. From the request-type dropdown, select "Request to Remove Information."
5. Enter your email address, and in the message box paste the profile URL you copied.
6. Submit the form; removal is reported to typically complete within about 48 hours.
7. If you have an AncientFaces account under your own name, you can alternatively delete it directly via https://www.ancientfaces.com/account#settings ("Delete Account").
8. Repeat separately for any additional matching profiles you find, since genealogy sites often have multiple entries for the same person across different records.
