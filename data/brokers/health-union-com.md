1. Open Health Union's Privacy Rights Request Form at https://privacyportal.onetrust.com/webform/c402d2db-7536-4212-a23d-ed4a33c1b3b2/e3aec736-7c0a-4313-a3e7-132ff601397a (it is also linked from https://health-union.com/privacy-choices/).
2. Choose the request type you want: opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, and, if you also want it, deletion of the data they hold about you.
3. Fill in the identifying details the form asks for (at least your name and email) and submit. Complete any verification step the form or a follow-up email asks for.
4. If the form won't load or you prefer email, write to privacy@health-union.com saying you want to opt out of the sale/sharing of your personal information and of targeted advertising, and (optionally) that you want your data deleted.
5. Health Union says it confirms receipt within 10 business days and responds within 45 days; if you hear nothing in 10 business days, email privacy@health-union.com.
6. On any Health Union community site you visit, also use "Manage Cookies" (or turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser) to switch off targeting cookies. This only covers that browser and device, so repeat it on each one.
