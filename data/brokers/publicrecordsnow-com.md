1. Go to `https://www.publicrecordsnow.com/static/view/optout/`.
2. Enter your first name, last name, city, and state exactly as they appear on your listing.
3. Complete the CAPTCHA/security check and click "Opt Out."
4. Check your email for a confirmation message and click the verification link to finalize the removal.
5. Allow 3-7 business days for the listing to clear. If the opt-out page or confirmation email doesn't work, note that PublicRecordsNow often mirrors data from PeopleFinders -- also submit an opt-out there, since removal from one does not guarantee removal from the other.
