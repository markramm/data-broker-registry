1. Go to the RocketReach profile-removal tool at https://rocketreach.co/remove-profile.
2. Search for your name or find the specific RocketReach profile URL that matches you.
3. Follow the form's prompts to submit a removal request; this same tool doubles as the "Do Not Sell or Share My Info" opt-out under US state privacy laws (CCPA/CPRA).
4. Provide an email address so RocketReach can confirm your request and locate any matching profile.
5. If you have Global Privacy Control (GPC) enabled in your browser, RocketReach states it will honor that as an automatic opt-out signal.
6. If the form doesn't work or you prefer email, send your removal/opt-out request directly to privacy@rocketreach.co, including your full name and any RocketReach profile URL(s) that match you.
7. RocketReach reports typically responding to removal/opt-out requests in under a day; if you don't get a response, follow up via the Data Protection Officer line at (833) 212-3828.
