1. Go to AudienceMix's "Opt Out Request" form at https://www.audiencemix.net/do-not-sell.
2. Enter your email address (required), and your first and last name so AudienceMix can match you. In "I am submitting this request on behalf of", choose "Myself".
3. In the message box, say which requests you are making: opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, and delete it if you want that too.
4. Submit the form and watch your inbox for a follow-up.
5. If the form doesn't work, email the same request to privacy@audiencemix.net.
