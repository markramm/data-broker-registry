1. Go to the RGR Marketing privacy policy page at https://www.rgrmarketing.com/privacy-policy/.
2. Note that RGR Marketing does not publish a dedicated self-service opt-out/deletion form; California residents' rights are handled by direct contact instead.
3. Email info@rgrmarketing.com and state that you are a California resident (or resident of another state with applicable privacy rights) exercising your right to know what categories of personal information RGR Marketing has shared about you with third parties, and request deletion/opt-out of further sale or sharing of your lead data.
4. Include your full name and the email address most likely associated with any lead inquiry (mortgage, solar, or home-improvement) submitted under your name, so RGR Marketing can locate your record.
5. If you only want to stop unrelated commercial emails, you can separately request removal from marketing email by replying to any commercial email you've received from RGR Marketing or emailing info@rgrmarketing.com.
6. RGR Marketing states it will verify your identity before acting on the request; expect a reply requesting confirmation details.
7. Because this is a lead-generation reseller with many downstream business "clients" who may have already purchased your lead data, consider this opt-out as stopping RGR's own future sharing -- it will not retroactively recall data already sold to a client before your request.
