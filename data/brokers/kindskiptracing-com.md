1. Go to https://kindskiptracing.com/opt-out (the "Opt-Out" link in the site footer).
2. Enter what you can so they can match your records. They need at least one of: your email address, your phone number, or your last name with your street address and ZIP code. Each extra detail makes a match more likely.
3. Submit the form. Kind Skiptracing says it will stop selling or sharing your information within 15 business days.
4. The form only covers selling and sharing. To ask for deletion too, email compliance@kindskiptracing.com (the contact on California's data broker registry) or info@kindskiptracing.com. Say you are asking for your personal information to be deleted, and under the CCPA if you live in California.
