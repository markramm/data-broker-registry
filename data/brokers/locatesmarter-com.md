1. Go to LocateSmarter's opt-out form at https://forms.locatesmarter.com/OptOutForm (linked from "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" in the locatesmarter.com footer).
2. Choose "I am submitting this request for myself."
3. Fill in your first and last name, email address, and your street address, city, state and ZIP code. Use the address LocateSmarter is most likely to have on file. Repeat with a second request for an older address if you've moved recently.
4. Check "Request to Stop Selling" and submit. The form says it doesn't need full identity verification for an opt-out of sale.
5. To ask for deletion as well, use the separate CCPA request-to-know-or-delete form at https://locatesmarter.com/ccpa-form/.
6. If the form won't submit, call LocateSmarter toll-free at 877-815-2268 (answered 8am–5pm Central) and make the same opt-out request by phone.
