1. Search your name on the site (it now operates under the publicrecordsnow.com domain) to find your listing, and note your city and state as they appear.
2. Go to https://publicrecordsnow.com/static/view/optout/.
3. Enter your first name, last name, city, and state exactly as they appear in your listing.
4. Complete the CAPTCHA and submit the opt-out request.
5. If the dedicated opt-out page is unavailable, use the site's "Contact Us" form instead and put "OPT ME OUT" in the subject line, including your name, city, and state in the message.
6. Expect processing to take roughly 7-14 days; re-check periodically since people-search sites re-ingest public records on a rolling basis.
