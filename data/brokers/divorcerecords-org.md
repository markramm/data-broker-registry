1. Go to the InfoTracer opt-out portal at https://infotracer.com/optout/ (divorcerecords.org shares this removal system with other InfoTracer-branded sites).
2. Search for your listing by name and state.
3. Select the matching record(s) from the search results.
4. Complete the CAPTCHA and submit the removal request with your email address.
5. Click the confirmation link InfoTracer emails you to finalize the opt-out.
6. If the portal is unresponsive or the listing persists, call 800-309-9352 and reference divorcerecords.org.
