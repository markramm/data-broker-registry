<!-- Real instructions based on Skip Smasher's public opt-out wizard, hotline, and privacy-page description. -->
1. Go to https://www.skipsmasher.com/Consumers/ and click the "Start" / "Know, Delete and Opt-Out" button, which opens the removal wizard (wizard.aspx).
2. Enter your full name and current address exactly as they'd appear in a Skip Smasher record, so the wizard can locate the correct listing(s).
3. Review any matching record(s) the wizard surfaces and select the one(s) that are you.
4. Submit the opt-out request through the wizard; if it fails or you'd rather not use the online tool, call the toll-free privacy hotline at 1-866-505-7170 or email info@skipsmasher.com with your name and address instead.
5. California residents can also use the "Do Not Share or Sell My Personal Information" link in the site footer as an alternate route to the same request.
6. Skip Smasher may take multiple business days to confirm; if your listing reappears, repeat the request -- like most people-search sites it rescrapes public records periodically.
