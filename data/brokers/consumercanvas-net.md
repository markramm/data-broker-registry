1. Go to the [Consumer Canvas privacy request form](https://privacyportal-eu.onetrust.com/webform/07a2e8c2-346a-4f58-865f-0d56c9baaff5/e6013ecc-3719-4f58-adb1-5121ea4797f8) (it is the "Submit a Privacy Request" link on consumercanvas.net/privacy).
2. Choose "U.S. Consumer", then enter your first and last name, email, country and state.
3. Choose one request type. Start with "Request to Delete My Personal Information".
4. Leave the details box empty or write a short note; the form asks you not to put personal information there. Type the CAPTCHA code and submit.
5. The form takes one request type per submission. Submit it again choosing "Opt Out of Sales, Sharing, or Targeted Advertising of My Personal Information" (and, if you want, "Request to Delete My Consumer Health Data").
6. Deletion requests are identity-checked, so watch your email for a verification message. As an alternative you can email privacy@consumercanvas.net with the subject line "Privacy Rights".
