1. Go to the [FamilyTreeNow opt-out page](https://www.familytreenow.com/optout).
2. Enter an email address and solve the CAPTCHA, then click "Begin Opt Out Procedure."
3. Search using your first name, middle name (if applicable), last name, city, state, and birth year.
4. Locate your matching record in the results and click the red "Opt Out This Record" button.
5. Check your email for a confirmation message from FamilyTreeNow and click the verification link -- removal is not final until you confirm.
6. If the CAPTCHA fails to load, try a different browser or clear your cache; removal can take up to 72 hours, and repeat for any additional matching records.
