1. Read the "Your Choices Regarding Your Personal Information" section of the privacy policy at `http://www.publicbackgroundchecks.com/privacypolicy.aspx` for the current request instructions.
2. Send a removal/deletion request by email to `info@backgroundchecks.com`, including your full name, email address, and phone number so they can locate and verify your listing.
3. Alternatively, call 866-300-8524 and make the same request by phone.
4. Reference CCPA (or your state's equivalent privacy law) if you want the request processed as a formal Do-Not-Sell/deletion request.
5. Expect processing within 5-10 business days; save your request confirmation and follow up if your listing hasn't been removed after two weeks.
