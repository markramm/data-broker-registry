1. Know before you start: publicbackgroundchecks.com itself is gone. The domain is parked -- every page on it redirects to an empty placeholder, and there is no opt-out form or privacy policy left to read. The steps below route to its parent company instead, and that routing is **not confirmed** for this defunct site.
2. Email support@backgroundchecks.com and ask them to access and delete the personal information they hold on you. This is the written channel the parent's own privacy policy names, under "Your Choices Regarding Your Personal Information."
3. Include your full name, email address, and phone number so they can locate a record, and state that you are exercising your CCPA (or your state's equivalent) right to deletion. Say explicitly that your request covers records sourced from publicbackgroundchecks.com, since the parent's policy does not mention that domain.
4. Expect friction, and do not read it as your mistake. The parent's privacy policy is written for employment screening -- it addresses "Applicants" and "Clients," names only www.backgroundchecks.com and app.backgroundchecks.com, and contains no data-broker opt-out language. The address above is documented there mainly for email unsubscribe requests. They may decline, or route you to an FCRA dispute process instead.
5. If they direct you to a different form, address, or consumer-rights portal, that is a genuinely useful finding -- please report it, because it is the piece this entry is missing.
6. Do not bother with the info@backgroundchecks.com address or the 866-300-8524 number that circulate in third-party removal guides for this site. Neither appears anywhere in the company's own privacy policy, and this entry no longer carries them.
