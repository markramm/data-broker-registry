1. Go to https://optout.porchgroupmedia.com/ (Porch Group Media's "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" form).
2. Choose the certification option that applies (usually "Myself").
3. Fill in your name and the postal address they are likely to have for you, plus your email. Phone is also asked for; include it only if you want that number removed too.
4. Enter today's date and click "Submit Form". The page says removal takes up to 10 business days.
5. If you have lived at several addresses recently, submit the form once for each address.
6. To ask for access to or deletion of your data rather than just an opt-out of sharing, use the "Privacy Choices" link in the site footer or email privacy@porchgroupmedia.com.
