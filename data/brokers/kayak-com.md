1. Go to https://www.kayak.com/privacy/manage.
2. Find the "Do not sell or share my info" toggle and switch it on -- no login or account is required to use this control.
3. Optionally, use the "Delete my data" button on the same page to request deletion of stored usage data (this may require signing in if tied to an account).
4. If you prefer, email privacy@kayak.com directly and state you are exercising your CCPA right to opt out of the sale/sharing of your personal information.
5. Changes to the toggle should take effect going forward; re-check periodically if you continue using KAYAK's site or apps.
