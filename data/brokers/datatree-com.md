1. Go to https://www.firstam.com/privacy-policy/ and look for the "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link, typically in the site footer -- this is First American's centralized privacy-request channel covering its DataTree product line.
2. Alternatively, call 1-866-718-0097 to submit your request by phone.
3. Or email dataprivacy@firstam.com directly, stating that you are requesting access, correction, or deletion of personal information (including any property-ownership records) associated with your name under CCPA, and specifically mention DataTree.
4. Be aware First American states it does not consider most of what it holds to be "sold" or "shared" in the CCPA sense, since property ownership records are largely drawn from public county recorder filings -- your practical ability to fully remove a property record may be limited, but you can still request correction or a formal response.
5. If you're a real-estate professional with a DataTree.com login, note that opt-out requests are handled through First American's general privacy channel above, not through the DataTree product login itself.
