Your State Privacy Rights
LexisNexis Risk Solutions
LexisNexis Risk Solutions Inc. and the LexisNexis Risk Solutions companies in scope of the U.S.
state data privacy laws (referred to throughout as the "State Privacy Laws") are providing the
information below for residents of California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Indiana, Iowa,
Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, Nevada, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon,
Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah and Virginia to exercise their rights pursuant to the State
Privacy Laws.

Exercise Your Rights Under Your State Privacy Laws
The State Privacy Laws grant residents certain rights with respect to their
personal information. These rights may be exercised by residents of states that
have State Privacy Laws through our online portal, U.S.
Mail, or over the phone.
Request A Copy of Your Personal Information
You may have the right to request a
State Privacy Act Report containing your personal information collected
and sold or disclosed subject to the requirements and application of your state
privacy law.
Notice of Right to Opt-Out Of Sale/Sharing
A Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information request, sometimes referred to as
an opt-out request, will restrict your personal information from being sold or
processed subject to the requirements and application of your state privacy law.
Our products and services are not linked to tracking technologies utilized
through our websites for targeted advertising, and we do not share personal
information in our products and services for targeted advertising online.
The interactive form to submit an opt-out request is available here:
Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information
California residents may also request to limit the use or disclosure of your
sensitive personal information here:
Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal
Information
Delete My Personal Information
You may have the right to request the deletion of your personal information
subject to the requirements and application of your state privacy law.
Right to Correct
You may have the right to request the correction of inaccurate personal
information maintained about you subject to the requirements and application of
your state privacy law. To submit a correction request, contact us via U.S. Mail
at the address below or call us at 1-888-217-1591 to speak to a live LexisNexis
Risk Solutions Consumer Center representative.
Right to Be Free from Discrimination
You have the right not to be discriminated against in pricing and services or retaliated against because you exercise any of your rights under the State Privacy Laws.
Website Cookies
This Consumer Portal does not use tracking technologies, such as cookies, for targeted advertising. Other LexisNexis Risk Solutions websites may use cookies, which may be considered a “sale” of your personal information for targeted advertising. You can opt out of the tracking technologies by selecting the “Cookie Settings” link on those sites or by sending an opt-out preference signal from your device or browser. Your use of an opt-out preference signal will only apply to the device or browser that you use and cannot opt-out consumer information that is not linked to your device or browser.
