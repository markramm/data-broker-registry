Opt Out
Important: Cross Pixel uses cookies to enable it's data collection technologies.
In order to maintain a user's opt out preference, Cross Pixel must set a cookie on the user's
browser. Disabling or deleting cookies - specifically Cross Pixel's opt out cookie - will cause the user's opt out
status to be lost. The user will then have to opt out again.
