Do Not Sell/Right to Opt-out
Depending on your state of residence and applicable law, you may have the right to request to opt out of the sale and sharing of your personal information.
Please carefully follow the directions below to submit a request to opt-out of the sale of your information.
Enter your email address and name and complete the verification below. We will send a link to your email address that will take you to the opt-out form.
Click the link sent to your email. It may take some time to arrive. If you wait more than 24 hours to click this link you will need to request a new one.
Enter your information on the form. This should match the information on the record you want removed.
You will be taken to a confirmation page verifying that we have received your request and it is being processed. You will also receive a confirmation email. Your request will be processed within the legally required timeframe. If your request is approved, please allow at least 3 days for your record to be removed from our site.
Note: Omitting information or providing inaccurate information on the opt-out form will only hinder the opt-out process. Please fill out the entire form accurately so that we can ensure we remove all your information from our site.
