The California Consumer Privacy Act ("CCPA") provides California residents with the right to opt out of the sale of their personal information (or “Personal Data”) by a business to third parties, subject to certain exceptions as permitted by law. Additional jurisdictions, including the European Union, through the General Data Protection Act, and the UK also provide data subjects with the ability to exercise certain rights over their information.
