Manage Mailings and Data
Looking to stop the delivery of your Save mailer? We’re sad to see you go. To complete your opt-out request, please visit our Privacy Rights Request page.
Please note that by checking any of the options on the page to delete information or opt-out, you will stop receiving our advertising. This includes the Save mailer, if you currently receive it.
If you change your mind and want to opt back in to receiving the grocery circulars, coupons, and valuable ads included in the Save mailer, please complete the form on the Join the Family page.
Please note that it can take up to six weeks for the mailings to stop as our advertising programs are prepared in advance of distribution. We appreciate your patience while we process your request.
