Intent IQ – Data Subject Rights
AVAILABLE RIGHTS: If you reside in a jurisdiction with an applicable privacy law that applies to Intent IQ and your personal information, then you have specific rights regarding the personal information that Intent IQ maintains about you. These rights apply in certain U.S. States (California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, Texas, Oregon, Montana, New Hampshire, Delaware, Iowa, Nebraska, New Jersey, Tennessee, Minnesota, Maryland, Indiana,Kentucky and Rhode Island), the United Kingdom, the European Union and certain other jurisdictions. The rights herein are only available to you if you are a resident of such jurisdictions and the laws of such jurisdiction apply to Intent IQ. These rights may include the right to access your information, the right to delete/erase your information, the right to correct/rectify your information, the right to opt-out of selling/sharing of your information, the right to opt-out of targeted advertising with your information, the right to object to our processing of your information, the right to revoke any consents, the right to limit the use of your sensitive personal information and other rights available under applicable laws and/or as set forth in our Technology Privacy Policy.
Please keep in mind that Intent IQ’s products and services do not include the collection and use of identifiable information such as your name, physical address or unhashed email address. Rather, Intent IQ’s data is used to identify a device and may consist of mobile device IDs (such as advertising IDs a/k/a “MAIDS”), cookies, IP addresses, user agents and hashed email addresses. We generally do not create targeting segments or make advertising targeting decisions based on your data. Rather, our primary business is to enable others, such as our clients, to make their own targeting decisions based upon the sites and services that you have accessed online. To learn more about our data processing activities, please see our Technology Privacy Policy.
EXERCISING RIGHTS *:Intent IQ uses, sells and shares the information we possess so that more relevant ads and other services can be delivered to you by third parties. While this information may be directly tied to your individual device (such as a computer or mobile device), this information may be “probabilistically” tied to your device. This means that we believe the information is tied to your device, but it may in fact be tied to other devices. Therefore, when you opt-out using the method below, it is possible that we will be opting out other devices in addition to yours that are on the same computer network with you.
