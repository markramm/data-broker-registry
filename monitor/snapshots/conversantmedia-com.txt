Consumer Privacy Rights
Epsilon provides a variety of marketing services to its clients, such as personalized online and offline advertising and licensing of consumer data.
Please review the Epsilon Privacy Policy for more information about how Epsilon collects, uses, discloses, and deletes consumer personal information.
Please use the form below to submit your requests. For US consumers who prefer not to submit your request online, please call +1-866-267-3861. Epsilon may keep a record of your request and our response to it as required by law.
If you are an Epsilon employee or have applied for a role with Epsilon, please send your request to privacyofficer@publicisgroupe.com.
If you are an agent submitting a request on behalf of a consumer, Epsilon requires additional written proof showing your authority as an agent or other legal entitled individual depending on local laws.
