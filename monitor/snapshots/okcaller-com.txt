General Questions
1.Do I need to create an account to use your reference directory?
You can browse public number pages, available historical directory information and community reports without an account. Sign in with Google, or use your existing password account, for current caller-name lookups and your private prior searches. New accounts use Google sign-in. An account does not remove the service’s lookup limits.
2. What countries are listed in your site?
The main web directory covers United States and Canadian numbers. International lookup and app coverage vary by number and provider.
3. Are businesses listed, or only home numbers?
OkCaller provides Caller Name ID for individuals and businesses.
4. Is there an app?
Check the current Caller-ID Global Lookup app-store listings for iOS or Android availability, caller-name lookup, and any premium address or email search features and prices.
5. Why can't I find a number I'm searching for?
Caller-name information is not available for every number, and a missing result is not a safety rating. A record may be missing, outdated or withheld, and a provider may be temporarily unavailable. A historical directory entry or recorded search is not proof of who currently uses the number.
6. How exactly does Web Caller Name ID Work?
Available caller-name records, historical directory information and user reports provide context for the number you enter. A returned name does not authenticate the caller, because caller ID can be spoofed. The Terms of Service contain the site’s patent and permitted-use statements.
Opt-Out
1. How do I remove my phone number from your website?
Detailed Opt-Out Procedure
To manage your own listing, start with a number you control and use the available verification process:
Via Carrier: Ask your telecommunications provider about its caller-name settings. A carrier change does not guarantee removal from every directory or an update to previously saved records.
Via SMS System: Open your number's listing, choose SMS or phone-call verification, request a PIN, and enter the received PIN on the website to complete the available listing-update or opt-out steps. Only request a PIN for a number you control.
If the verification process fails, email info@okcaller.com with the number’s listing address and a description of the error. Support requests do not replace verification that you control the number.
Keep verification PINs and account passwords private. When requesting help, include the listing address and the problem you encountered, but do not email a PIN or password.
Payment & Fees
1. How much does OkCaller cost to use?
OkCaller is a free service. We work hard to maintain free Web Caller Name ID, and the service is sponsored by Google AdSense.
2. How does your information compare to paid "Reverse Search" services?
OkCaller provides available Web Caller Name ID for United States and Canadian numbers; coverage is not complete. Results can include caller-name provider records, historical directory information and community reports; these sources have different coverage and may be outdated. We do not charge for the web lookup. In some cases a paid search may have information not on our site, such as email, address, and age, but in other cases, a paid search may yield less information than our site. We recommended first searching OkCaller before you pay for premium services. Those services often derive from credit bureau and other proprietary data, and the monthly fees cover large marketing and data sourcing costs.
