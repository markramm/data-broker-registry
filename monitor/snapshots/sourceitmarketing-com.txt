FAQ
Question: What information do you keep on each consumer record?
Answer: We primarily deal with email data for businesses, But for consumers records may contain the following information: email, first name, last name, phone number (landline/mobile), gender, full address with (city, state, zip, county, country), age, IP address, and consumer interest categories.
Business data includes: job title, company name, company location.
Question: Where do you get your data?
Answer: The email contact lists that we have is from other third parties/data brokers, which include but not limited to L2 Data, Apollo, DatabaseUSA, Zoominfo, LinkedIn, GetPropsects, ExactData. We do not operate websites that collect consumer email lists.
Question: Data Removal?
Answer: After checking for your data record on this website. If found you will be emailed a link. After clicking the link you have the option to view and remove your data. If removed the data is wiped completely from our database and we only retain a one way hash of the data record to prevent data from being readded in the future.
Question: Data Removal in bulk
Answer: If you are a privacy company and looking to remove records in bulk contact us for an API. Please do not email us for every single request as that places a burden on us.
