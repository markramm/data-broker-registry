California Consumer Notice of Right to Opt-out of Sale/Sharing of Personal Information
California residents have the right, at any time, to direct Salutary Data not to sell or share their Personal Information.
If you wish to exercise your right to opt-out of the sale and/or sharing of your Personal Information, please provide your name, work email address, current employer name, and employer address in the form below. We will use this information to search our systems and exclude your personal information from future selling and/or sharing with third parties.
Salutary Data will cease the sale and/or sharing of your Personal Information to third parties no later than 15 business days from receipt of your request.
