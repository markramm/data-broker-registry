Overview
Some states in the U.S. provide residents with the right to opt out of the “sale,” “sharing,” or use of their personal data for “targeted advertising.” We process your personal information for various purposes, some of which may be deemed by the laws in these states as a “sale” or “sharing” of your personal data or as “targeted advertising.” Veeva Systems Inc. is a registered data broker in the U.S. states of California, Oregon, Texas, and Vermont.
Information about our practices and your choices can be found below. If you choose to exercise your rights, we will respond to your request in accordance with applicable laws, which may vary depending on where you live.
For more information on our privacy practices, please see our Privacy Notice.
Consumer-Based Advertising

More information about how we use personal information can be found here. For more information about tailored advertising and cross-device linking, please visit the Network Advertising Initiative (“NAI”) website. We are an active member of the NAI in good standing.
