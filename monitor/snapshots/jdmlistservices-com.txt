California Delete Act Compliance
Starting August 1, 2026, California consumers may submit a single, universal deletion request through the CPPA’s deletion mechanism. Upon receiving such a request from the CPPA, we will delete the consumer’s personal information from our systems and notify any service providers or contractors to do the same within 45 days, unless an exception applies under California law.
Audits
Beginning January 1, 2028, we will undergo an independent audit of our compliance with California’s data broker laws every three years, and beginning January 1, 2029, we will submit confirmation of audit completion to the CPPA.
3. How We Use Information
We use the limited personal information we receive solely to facilitate transactions between our clients and independent data vendors, and to perform administrative functions such as billing and tracking campaign results.
4. How We Share Information
We do not sell personal information for our own marketing purposes. We may share personal information with:
• Clients, to fulfill their orders for marketing data.
• Vendors, to obtain the requested marketing data.
• Service providers, to support administrative operations.
We require that all parties receiving personal information comply with applicable privacy laws.
5. Data Retention
We retain personal information only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was provided or as required by law.
6. Data Security
We apply administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect the limited personal information we process.
7. Your Privacy Rights – Overview
Depending on your state of residence, you may have the right to:
• Access, correct, or delete your personal information.
• Obtain a portable copy of your personal information.
• Opt out of targeted advertising, the sale of personal information, or profiling.
• Appeal a decision regarding your privacy request.
8. How to Exercise Your Rights
You can submit a request by emailing privacy@jdmlistservices.com or calling 1-888-526-0909. We will verify your identity before processing your request and respond within the timeframes required by law.
