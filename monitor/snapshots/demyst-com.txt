Personal Information Request Form for California Residents
To exercise your rights under the California Consumer Protection Act ("CCPA"), please submit a verifiable consumer request to us by filling out the following form or emailing us with the information requested in the form below at privacy@demystdata.com. Please note, only you, or a person who you authorize to act on your behalf, may make a verifiable consumer request related to your personal information.
Demyst Data will not discriminate against you for exercising any of your CCPA rights.
You may also opt out of the sharing or "sale" of your Personal Information by selecting the "Opt out of the sale of my Personal Information" option below consistent with applicable law. Please note, California’s definition of "sale" includes transfers that ordinarily may not be thought of as a "sale" in the traditional sense – such as sharing information with our affiliates.
