Your Privacy Choices - About LiftBase Data
Your Privacy Choices
Your request will be applied to all personal information Blue Hill Marketing Solutions, Inc. (BHMS) holds across its brands and data products, including those operated under the LiftEngine and LaunchPad names. Our records are organized by U.S. residential address. If you have never had a residential address in the United States, we do not hold information about you. Additionally, we do not hold information on minors.
What Deletion Can and Can’t Do
Deleting your information stops us from sharing it from now on. Deleting does not affect what’s been shared prior to your request. See our Privacy Policy (last updated Sep. 21, 2026) for more information.
