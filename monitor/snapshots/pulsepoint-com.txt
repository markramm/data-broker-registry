Consumer Opt Out Tool
As described in our Platform Privacy Policy, we collect certain information that may be deemed “Personal Information” or “Personal Data” under applicable laws. We collect and use this data for activity that may constitute “selling,” “sharing,” “targeted advertising” or “cross-context behavioral advertising” under those applicable laws. If you wish to opt out of our use of your Personal Information/Data for these purposes, you can use this tool and click the link below. This opt out tool will set an opt out cookie or “flag” that will, effectively, tell us not to use your data for the above mentioned purposes.
Keep in mind that:
Deleting browser cookies may remove your opt-out preferences. If you delete or clear your cookies, you may need to re-set or resubmit your opt out.
Cookie-based opt outs are browser and device specific. If you use more than one browser or device, you will need to opt out on every browser that you use.
If your browser's settings block cookies, you may experience unsuccessful opt-out requests and may see a message that an "Opt-Out is Blocked by Browser Settings," or that an "Opt-Out is Temporarily Unavailable."
Opting out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising or cross contextual behavioral advertising does not mean you will no longer see advertising online. It does mean that the company or companies from which you opt out will no longer show ads that have been tailored to your interests. You may still see ads served by PulsePoint but they will not be targeted based on your Personal Information/Data.
