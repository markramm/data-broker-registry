Welcoming Fetcher’s Customers to Juicebox
Today, we're sharing that Juicebox has reached an agreement to acquire Fetcher's assets. As Fetcher retires its platform over the coming weeks, we'll be here to welcome their customers and help them keep their hiring moving.
The partnership reflects Juicebox and Fetcher's shared ambition to help companies and recruiters find top talent.
What Fetcher built
Fetcher pioneered human-in-the-loop recruiting software in 2016.
Over the past decade, the team raised over $30 million, helped 1,500+ companies build exceptional teams, and empowered 5,000+ recruiters to source and engage candidates with less manual work.
Their approach paired technology with personalized support, giving recruiters more time for the part of the job that matters most: building relationships with talent.
We have a lot of respect for what they built and for the care they've shown their customers along the way.
"Juicebox has been on our radar for years. I believe they have the most compelling product on the market right now, and their platform offers the smoothest transition for our customers," said Chris Calmeyn, CEO of Fetcher.
We're proud to carry that mission forward and offer Fetcher customers a path to what comes next, with a larger product, advanced agentic capabilities, and a world-class support team to ensure their success.
For Fetcher Customers
Effective immediately, Fetcher’s customers can move to Juicebox. Fetcher’s customers, spanning critical industries and collectively representing hundreds of recruiters, will be able to benefit from Juicebox’s leading AI-native recruiting solution.
We know this transition comes on a short timeline, especially for teams with searches in progress. Our team is ready to help you get set up quickly so there's no gap in your sourcing.
"Our goal is to build on the strong relationships that Fetcher has built by continuing to offer these customers world-class an AI-native recruiting solutions," said David Paffenholz, CEO & Co-Founder of Juicebox.
Fetcher customers can learn more about their options, including an exclusive offer available through November 19, here or you can schedule time directly with our team to learn more.
To the Fetcher customers and community, we'd love to welcome you into this next chapter and continue building the future of recruiting together.
David Paffenholz
Co-Founder & CEO
David Paffenholz is the Founder & CEO of Juicebox (PeopleGPT), an AI-powered recruiting platform trusted by over 5,000 customers. He’s worked closely with founders, AI labs, and high-growth companies to design modern hiring systems and win competitive talent markets.
