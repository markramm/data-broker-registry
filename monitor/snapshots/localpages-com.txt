Privacy Policy
Privacy is important to us at Local Pages. This Privacy Policy describes what information we gather, how we use it, and what choices you have regarding your information while visiting our website.
By accessing or using our site, you give your consent to the terms detailed below. If you do not wish to use these terms, kindly do not use our site.
Information We Collect
We may collect two types of information:
Non-Personal Information
Such non-personal information includes the following data that does not directly identify you:
Browser type and its version
Device type with its Operating system
Referring website.
Pages viewed and time spent on the website
Geographical location with respect to the broad area like city or state.
Knowledge concerning how participants use Local Pages aids users and transforms the content and user interface, thus improving this information.
Voluntarily Provided Information.
In the event that you contact us via email or subscribe to updates, we may collect personal data such as name with the email address.
We do not request or keep records of highly confidential private information such as payment methods along addresses, or social security numbers.
How We Use Your Information?
Service Provision
The information you provide to us enables us to meet your content and service needs. This ranges from providing feedback you may ask from us, sending newsletters or updates you have subscribed to, and enhancing your interaction with our site by making it more appealing to you.
Enhancing The Site
Studying or analyzing the data collected from users allows us to gain understanding of secured behaviors and our preferences. This further guides us in making decisions for the advancement of our websites, their contents and services as users may find more appealing. We can meet the most relevant and changing needs of our users.
Adhering To The Law
For the protection of the services we provide and the services put online, your personal data provided may be needed. Responding to legal actions, stopping fraud and caring for the safety of our sites and services are examples why we may need such information.
Opting Out
You have the right to opt-out of certain communications and data collection. If you no longer wish to receive newsletters or updates from us, you may unsubscribe at any time in accordance with the directions given in the communication or by reaching out to us.
Protecting Personal Data
We regard your information as secure and take appropriate actions to protect it against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No system for transmitting or storing information is ever entirely secure. We aim to protect your personal information to the highest reasonable standard.
Modifications to this Policy
This Privacy Policy may be edited from time to time for changes in our procedures or due to business, legal, or regulatory reasons. Any updates will be reflected in this document and, therefore, we urge you to review it from time to time so that you understand how your information is being secured.
Get in Touch With Us
In case you have any issues relating to this policy or the way we are handling data, do not hesitate to reach out via email at contact@localpages.com. Your queries will be dealt with, and your privacy shall be maintained.
