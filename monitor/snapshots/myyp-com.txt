Why?
Our goal is to connect local customers with local businesses, whether through directories in print, on the Internet, or in a mobile app. While many people still rely on print directories to make local purchase decisions, Yellow Pages publishers know that there is no point in delivering a print directory to someone who does not want one. That is why we joined together to create YellowPagesOptOut.com to give consumers the choice to limit or stop the delivery of print directories to their homes.
