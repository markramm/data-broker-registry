Your Privacy Matters to Us
At Brooks Integrated Marketing (BrooksIM), we are committed to respecting your privacy and complying with all applicable data protection laws.
As a registered data broker in California and other states, we do not engage directly with individual consumers, nor do we compile data independently. We recognize the importance of giving individuals control over their personal information and the right to delete, opt-out or access their data.
In recent years, we've seen a significant rise in opt-out and deletion requests—from 145 requests in 2023 to 68,593 in 2024. In response, we’ve optimized our privacy request process to ensure most requests are fulfilled within minutes.
How to Submit a Privacy Request
We offer four convenient options for submitting a request to opt out or delete your personal data:
1. Online Form (Preferred Method)
Complete our [Data Subject Access Request Form] below — the fastest and most secure way to submit your information directly.
2. Email Request
Send your request to privacy@brooksim.com. For quicker processing, please include your information using the format provided in our sample (below).
3. Phone Request
Call our dedicated privacy line at 800-531-2601 x998.
4. Bulk Requests (For Third Parties)
If you’re submitting multiple requests on behalf of others, we recommend using our API. To request an API key for JSON POST access, email datascientist@brooksim.com.
Once you fill out the form below you will receive an email to confirm your submission at the email provided. If you are a third-party, please contact web@brooksim.com to get api access for bulk requests that does not require this confirmation.
