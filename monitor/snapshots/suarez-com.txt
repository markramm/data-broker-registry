At SCI we are committed to safeguarding all consumer information. Because your privacy is important to us, we have developed this Privacy Policy to share with you how we protect the information that we collect from consumers. We invite you to continue reading to learn more about our information use practices and to learn how you can limit information about you that we may share with our affiliated companies and divisions and with unaffiliated third parties. If after you finish reading our Privacy Policy you wish to limit information about you that we may share with other members of the SCI family of companies or unaffiliated third parties, you will not learn about all of the opportunities that may be available to you.
SCI’S PRIVACY POLICY APPLIES TO YOU
The information in this Privacy Policy applies to individual customers that purchase our products and/or services, consumers that enter sweepstakes offerings and people from whom we obtain personally identifiable information when they initiate contact with us.
WHY WE GATHER INFORMATION
We gather information about consumers so that we are able to provide you with affordable, quality products and/or services. This information also helps us to be better able to provide attentive customer service.
SHARING INFORMATION WITH AFFILIATED AND UNAFFILIATED COMPANIES
From time to time we may share select consumer information with affiliated companies and divisions and with unaffiliated third parties whose products or services we think you may enjoy. If you do not want us to share your name and postal address, e-mail address or telephone number with either affiliated companies and divisions or unaffiliated third parties, please read the “How To Limit The Sharing Of Your Information” section below.
FOR OUR INTERNET CUSTOMERS
We collect the domain name and e-mail address of visitors to our web pages, when possible, the e-mail address of those who communicate with us via e-mail, aggregate information on what web pages consumers visit and information volunteered by visitors such as survey information and/or web site registrations. We use this information to improve the content of our web pages, to customize the content and/or layout of our pages for individual visitors, to notify consumers about updates to our web pages, to contact consumers for marketing purposes and to share the same information with unaffiliated third parties so that they may provide future offers for interesting products and services for you.
We always use encryption technology that is standard in the industry when receiving and transferring consumer information exchanged with our sites.
HOW TO LIMIT THE SHARING OF YOUR INFORMATION
If you do not want to receive periodic mailings, e-mails and/or telephone calls from either SCI and its affiliated companies and divisions OR unaffiliated third parties, please let us know whether you are declining to accept just opportunities from unaffiliated third parties or from SCI and its affiliated companies and divisions as well. If you are not specific, we will conclude that you are only requesting that your information not be shared with unaffiliated third parties and we will continue to provide you with information about the affordable, quality products and services offered by SCI and its affiliated companies and divisions. You can notify us by contacting us directly.
