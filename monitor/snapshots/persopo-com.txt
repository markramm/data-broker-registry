Opt Out
Persopo.com gathers records from federal, state, county and municipal databases. We provide
these records to our customers to be able to get the most current updated results available.
However, we are more than willing to remove anything that you would consider inconvenient to
be out there. The process is very simple, and you do not even have to be a member to Opt Out!
Step 1: Just do a search for yourself.
Step 2: Find the listing that matches
you by name, age, location, and/or possible relatives.
Step 3: Copy the entire
listing in text format (please no screenshots or links).
Step 4: Send it to us!
This can be done by e-mail or regular mail.
Step 5: Relax and let us remove the
results from our site.
Please be aware it may take anywhere from 10-20 business days to complete this request,
depending on how many requests are being processed at that time. Once your Opt Out
request has been completed we will additionally add your name to an ever-growing list that
we maintain with our data providers to try and not add your information in the future. Also
keep in mind that if new information is forwarded to us that our computers are unable to
match to your previous requests, those new records will be added to our database. Being
unable to match could be something as simple as a different middle initial, different
spelling of a name, or even if you should move to a different state. However, if you
discover this has happened, you may send us another request and we will remove the new
updated information on our list of people not to display on our site.
