Privacy Statement
We take your privacy seriously!
Spectrum Mailing Lists does not compile, store, publish or sell Personal Information relating to consumers. The only Consumer information compiled by Spectrum Mailing Lists is publicly available information that is from federal, state, or local government records. Simply complete the opt-out form, and we will delete your name & address from our data if it currently exists, as well as put your information into our perpetual suppression file in case you appear in the future.
