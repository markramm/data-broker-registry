Control of Your Personal Information
DataPartners believes that consumers should be in ultimate control over how and whether their personal information is used. This page describes all of your options regarding how you can regulate your personal information that may be possessed by DataPartners.
Our Privacy Policy
Our Privacy Policy describes in detail exactly how we collect data, what we do with it, and all of your options to stop or limit our use of your personal information. Click HERE to review our full Privacy Policy. Our Privacy Policy also includes specific subsections that address privacy laws applicable to residents of the certain states. Please review the subsection applicable to your state of residence.
Opting Out
If you would like to opt out of the sale of your personal data, targeted advertising, and profiling (i.e., prevent us from selling, sharing, or using your personal information), click HERE and fill out the required information. LIMIT THE USE OF MY SENSITIVE PERSONAL INFORMATION (RESIDENTS OF CERTAIN STATES ONLY)
You may be a resident of a state that gives consumers the right to limit the use of any of your sensitive personal information collected by us to certain uses specified by statute. However, DataPartners does not use consumer information for any of the purposes stated in those statutes. Accordingly, your closest alternative option is to exercise your right to opt out by clicking HERE or exercise your right have your information deleted by clicking HERE or calling us at toll-free at 866-423-1818 and speaking with our Consumer Privacy Coordinator who will assist you.
