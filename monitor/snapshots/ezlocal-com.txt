"Personal Information" means information that you provide to us that personally identifies you, such as your first and last name, email address, street address, zip code, phone number, or billing information.
"Non-Personal Information" means information that does not specifically identify you.
Information you provide to us.
We collect information that you provide to us. For example, our Service may require that you register for an EZlocal Account to receive certain features of our Service. Registration may include your disclosure to us of Personal Information. Whenever you send us personal correspondence or when other users or third parties send us correspondence about your activities or postings on the Site, we may collect and store such information. We may also receive information from you if you enter our promotions or sweepstakes, or if you participate in chat messages or contributions through other services available to you. Postings, activities, correspondences, and any content that you enter into the Service are governed by our Terms of Use.
Businesses who wish to be included in our business directory, or participate in our Service in any way may be asked to provide information such as a business name, website url, telephone number, physical address, email address, description of business, service areas, business category and/or keyword information, credit card information, and other contact information (collectively, "Business Information"). Some Business Information may be Personal Information. If this information is collected by us via telephone, you acknowledge that we may be recording your telephone call for our training or quality control purposes during such call.
Information we gather from your use of our Site.
We collect information about what parts of our Site and Service you use and how you use them. Information we may gather includes, but is not limited to:
Computer or browser information such as your system activity, the domain name of the website from which you linked to our Site, the time and duration of your use of our Service and any queries, browser type, and browser language. Cookies and Web Beacons
When you visit an EZlocal web page or Service, a cookie that uniquely identifies your browser may be sent to your computer or mobile device. A “cookie” is a small file containing your preferences that is sent to your browser and sent back by your browser each time your browser accesses our servers. EZlocal may set and access cookies on your computer to provide you with customized content. Most cookies are “session cookies,” which automatically delete themselves from your hard drive after each session. You are always free to decline these cookies if your browser permits, however, you may not be able to use certain features.
Cookies that we may use may only be read by the server that placed them there. We use cookies to improve the quality of our Service (for example, by fulfilling your language, filtering and other preferences) and to better understand how people interact with our Site. Most browsers are initially set up to accept cookies. You can reset your browser to refuse all cookies or to indicate when a cookie is being sent. However, some aspects of the Site may not function properly without cookies.
Like many Internet companies, EZlocal may automatically track information based upon your behavior on our Site. We use this information for research purposes to better understand, protect and service our customers. The information we track may include such information as browser type, internet service provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, platform type, date/time stamp, IP address and number of clicks to analyze trends, administer the Sites, track user’s movement in the aggregate, and gather broad demographic information for aggregate use. EZlocal respects your privacy and this information is not linked to any personally identifiable information, except as may be needed to prevent fraud or abuse of the Site. EZlocal works with third parties to serve ads on our Sites. These ads may contain cookies and/or action tags (also known as single pixel gifs or web beacons) to measure advertising effectiveness and gather completely anonymous information. We have no access to or control over these cookies or action tags.
We currently use the services of web analytics companies to help us measure the effectiveness of our Service, including the advertising on the Service. To do this, we allow the web analytics companies to include web beacons and cookies on business profiles and search results pages. The type of information collected via such web beacons and cookies includes search terms, search parameters, click-throughs by users, and other similar information. By supplementing our records, this information helps us learn things like user preferences, popular search categories, click-through rates, how to improve our Service, and what kinds of offers our users like to see. Although our web analytics companies log this information on our behalf, we control how that data may be used.
Most, if not all, of the advertisements appearing on the Service are delivered to you by one of our web advertising partners. In the course of delivering such advertisements, our web advertising partners may place or recognize a unique cookie on your browser, or place web beacons, JavaScript or other similar technologies on our Service for the purpose of collecting information. The information collected by our web advertising partners through cookies, web beacons or other similar technologies is anonymous, and our web advertising partners are not authorized to collect any Personal Information about you.
We use Stripe for payment, analytics, and other business services. Stripe collects identifying information about the devices that connect to its services. Stripe uses this information to operate and improve the services it provides to us, including for fraud detection. Your basic Google account identifier, so we can link connections made by the same Google login.
How we use it: only to provide and improve the reporting and management features you requested in your EZlocal dashboard. It is shown only to you and to EZlocal staff supporting your account.
What we don't do: we do not sell Google user data, share it with third parties (except as needed to operate the service or as required by law), use it for advertising, or use it to train AI or machine-learning models. We never change your Google Analytics or Google Ads settings or data.

You can also remove EZlocal's access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of stored data, contact us at privacy@ezlocal.com.
