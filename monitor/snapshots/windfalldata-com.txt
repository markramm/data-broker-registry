Your Privacy Choices
Privacy choices for residents of states with applicable privacy laws
Customers and consumers who reside in states with applicable privacy laws are entitled to exercise certain privacy rights regarding their personal information as described in our Privacy Policy.
Please note that, in order to account for multiple instances of common names, our database is organized by name and address. Accordingly, we need the following information to be able to process your opt-out: (a) first and last name, and (b) current address. We also need your email address to notify you about the status of your request. We will use the information you provide when you submit a request only to facilitate your request and to maintain a record of our response for legal and compliance purposes.
Opt out of the sale of personal information to our Customers
Consumers can exercise the right to opt out of the “sale” of your personal information (as defined by applicable law) at any time by submitting the form below.
By opting out with Windfall, you’re opting out of Windfall’s databases only, not databases belonging to other companies, including our Customers.
Right to opt out of targeted advertising
As described in our Privacy Policy, to help us market and advertise our services, we may use cookies and other technologies on our website or otherwise share personal information with advertising partners in a manner that may be considered “selling”, “sharing” or “targeted advertising” under applicable laws. You can opt out by declining cookies. Individuals in California and Colorado can also opt out by enabling the Global Privacy Control setting within the browser that you use to access our services. Learn more at the Global Privacy Control website. Please note that your opt out will be specific to the device and browser you use when you opt out.
Request to access, correct, or delete personal information
Where provided by applicable laws, you can request to access a copy of your personal information, correct personal information that is inaccurate or out of date, and delete your personal information. In order for us to respond to your request, we may request additional information from you to prove your identity. For example, for requests to access your personal information, you must submit a phone number that matches the information we have in our records. If we cannot successfully verify your identity, we may not be able to provide you with your requested information. You may appeal our response to your request by submitting a request to appeal in the form below.
Authorized agents
Applicable law may allow you to use an “authorized agent” to submit requests on your behalf. If you have elected to use an authorized agent, or if you are an authorized agent who would like to submit requests on an individual’s behalf, please submit the request via the Exercise Privacy Rights icon below.
