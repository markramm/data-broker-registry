Domain Update — PeekYou.com
The domain name peekyou.com is now controlled by Atlas Data Privacy Corporation (“Atlas”).
On April 30, 2025, Atlas and three individual plaintiffs filed a lawsuit in New Jersey Superior Court: Atlas Data Privacy Corporation, et al. v. PeekYou LLC, et al. In the Complaint, Atlas asserted claims assigned to it by approximately 21,700 law enforcement officers, prosecutors, and other “covered persons” under New Jersey’s Daniel’s Law, which protects home addresses and unpublished home telephone numbers from disclosure.
Atlas and PeekYou LLC have entered into a settlement agreement, and this lawsuit has now been resolved.
Additional information regarding this and other Daniel’s Law cases can be obtained from the Superior Court of New Jersey.
