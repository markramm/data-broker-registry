MaxMind IP Address Opt-Out
What is the IP Address Opt-Out?
As outlined in MaxMind's Privacy Policy, certain laws and regulations
may give you the right to opt out of the sale of your personal
information by a business to third parties, subject to certain
exceptions as permitted by law. MaxMind may also provide you with the
ability to exercise similar options even where it is not legally
obligated to do so. To submit an opt-out request, please fill out and
submit the form below on this page. For more information about our
privacy practices,
click here.
MaxMind's IP address intelligence never identifies a household or
street address
MaxMind's IP address intelligence products associate IP addresses with
a postal code or larger geographic region, but never identify a
household or street address. When MaxMind provides a latitude and
longitude location, it is a point in or close to the applicable postal
code or larger geographic region but in a public location such as a
lake or public park. The accuracy radius of this data is 5km or more.
Learn more about MaxMind's Safe Coordinates program.
Submitting an Opt-Out Request may lead to service interruptions
As MaxMind's IP address intelligence services are used by thousands
of businesses worldwide, please be aware that opting out of sale
from MaxMind's services may mean that online businesses might be
unable to determine where you are located, and in some cases they
may block logins or other features that may impede your use of their
service. For example, you might be blocked from logging into a
password manager or from streaming certain content online. If you
choose to opt out of the sale, you may submit a request to opt in.
Please contact MaxMind directly at
privacy@maxmind.com
to submit an opt-in request.
