Firm Offers of Credit and Insurance (Prescreen)
The Fair Credit Reporting Act allows LexisNexis Risk Solutions Inc. to include your applicable information on prescreen lists used by creditors and insurers for the purpose of sending you firm offers of credit or insurance. You can elect to have your applicable information removed from such prescreen lists compiled by LexisNexis Risk Solutions Inc. by requesting to Opt-Out.
Removing your applicable information from prescreen lists does not affect your ability to apply for or obtain credit or insurance.
While your applicable information will be removed from the prescreen lists that LexisNexis Risk Solutions Inc. provides to businesses for the purpose of making a firm offer of credit or insurance, you may continue to receive offers from sources that do not use LexisNexis Risk Solutions Inc. to compile their prescreen lists.
Please note that opting out of LexisNexis Risk Solutions Inc. prescreen lists for firm offers of credit and insurance does not opt you out of Equifax, TransUnion, Experian, or other third-party lists. To also remove your applicable information from the Equifax, TransUnion, or Experian lists, call 1-888-567-8688.
Direct Marketing Services
LexisNexis Risk Solutions FL Inc. provides direct marketing services to customers to help their marketing campaigns reach consumers who are more likely to be interested in receiving such information. We remove consumers from these services upon request. Opting out of direct marketing services will prevent your applicable information from being used in unsolicited communications from LexisNexis Risk Solutions FL Inc.’s direct marketing products used by its customers.
Please note our company is not the only direct marketing company in the industry. Opting out of our LexisNexis Risk Solutions FL Inc. databases will not prevent other companies from sending you direct marketing materials or making telemarketing calls. Opting out of Direct Marketing Services also does not apply to Fair Credit Reporting Act Prescreen Opt-Outs, which are discussed separately below.
The Direct Marketing Association (DMA) also provides individuals with a form to use to have their applicable information removed from other direct marketers’ mail, e-mail, and telephone call lists. LexisNexis Risk Solutions, FL Inc. honors Opt-Out requests made through the DMA’s services. We also comply with national Do-Not-Call (DNC) requirements and honor Opt-Out requests through that service. To add your applicable information to the national DNC list, you may register at donotcall.gov. Many states have also created DNC lists for their residents.
Request for Information Suppression (Opt-Out) due to Public Safety
Individuals may request to Opt-Out of having personal information about themselves made available through certain LexisNexis Risk Solutions FL Inc. products and services, in accordance with legal requirements or if permitted by LexisNexis Risk Solutions FL Inc. policy. Such requests are referred to as "Information Suppressions." LexisNexis Risk Solutions FL Inc. permits individuals to have certain personal information about themselves suppressed from LexisNexis Risk Solutions FL Inc.'s public records.
Public and elected officials, including judges or judicial officers and law enforcement officers, and private individuals who are at risk due to threat of personal safety or where allowed by law, may request to have personal information about themselves suppressed from LexisNexis Risk Solutions FL Inc. products and services available to subscribers of our restricted public records products.
These products are available to commercial and government entities that meet credentialing requirements and are used to detect and prevent fraud, enforce transactions, perform due diligence, and other critical business and government functions. LexisNexis Risk Solutions FL Inc. generally does not suppress personal information from products or services that are available to law enforcement entities and products regulated by the Fair Credit Reporting Act. This only applies to personal information that is available through LexisNexis Risk Solutions FL Inc. -owned databases.
Please note that Information Suppressions applied to LexisNexis Risk Solutions FL Inc. databases will not prevent other companies or public records agencies from collecting or disseminating personal information. Requests for suppression from restricted public records products must include documentation substantiating the risk of personal safety or reason for the suppression request. LexisNexis Risk Solutions FL Inc. reserves the right to determine, in its sole reasonable discretion, what documentation meets this criteria, except where certain requirements may be imposed by law. If your suppression request is not approved, we will promptly inform you about that determination.
If you are a judge or judicial officer, law enforcement officer or public official, submit documentation substantiating your role or a letter from your supervisor stating that your position exposes you to a threat of personal safety; or,
If your safety is at risk and you are Not a public official or you do Not work in law enforcement, submit a copy of a court protective order, a copy of a police report, or similar documentation such as a letter from a shelter administrator or a health care professional.
All suppression requests along with supporting documents may be submitted using the "Request Opt-Out/Opt-In" button below.

Daniel's Law Privacy Protections
Daniel's Law allows covered persons including public and elected officials, judges or judicial officers, law enforcement officers, and their family members to restrict certain personal information from being made available subject to legal requirements. To request an Opt Out under Daniel's Law, please contact the Consumer Center at 888 497 0011 for assistance.
