Submit a Request Online
You can request information about yourself by filling out this secure form. To help us verify your identity, you'll need to provide proper identification, including your name, address, date of birth, and other details. For some requests, like getting your consumer disclosure report, we may also ask for your Social Security number or your driver's license number and state. Providing as much information as possible helps us process your request quickly.
We use the information you provide only to confirm your identity and complete your request. We do not sell or share your information with other companies. If we're unable to verify your identity or match your details to our records, we may not be able to complete your request.
If you have questions or need to upload documents, visit the LexisNexis Risk Solutions Reports Help page. If you'd like to speak with someone directly, call our Consumer Center at 1-888-497-0011.
For step-by-step guidance, review the Online Request Form Instructions . If you prefer to submit your request by mail, download the Printable Request Form and Printable Request Form Instructions. Please fill out all sections of the form so we can properly process your request. If we're unable to fulfill your request, we'll let you know by U.S. Mail.

2. Select Your Options
Request Your Consumer Disclosure Report
Select the Request Your Consumer Disclosure Report checkbox above to request a copy of your information. Under your state's privacy laws, you will receive both your LexisNexis® Risk Solutions Consumer Disclosure Report pertaining to the Fair Credit Reporting Act and a State Privacy Act Report pertaining to your state's privacy laws.
Request a Description of Procedure Letter
Select the Request a Description of Procedure Letter checkbox above to receive a letter from LexisNexis that is personal to your dispute request(s) and describes how we process dispute(s) that you may have in the system.
Select Your Data Privacy Act Options
As a resident under your state's data privacy laws you may exercise your privacy rights below:
Full Opt-Out:
Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information
A Full Opt-Out request will restrict your personal information from being sold to a third party subject to certain exceptions. In any case, an opt-out request will not apply to information that is exempt from the requirements of State Privacy Laws. For more information, or for additional methods to submit an opt-out request, see our Privacy Rights page.
When you visit our websites, we sometimes use tracking technologies which may be considered a "sale" of your personal information for targeted advertising. You may request to opt-out of such tracking technologies by utilizing the "Cookie Settings" link on our websites that use tracking technologies for targeted advertising or by sending an opt-out preference signal supported by your device or browser. Your use of an opt-out preference signal will apply only to your device or browser and not to other consumer information that is not linked to your device or browser. Please note this Consumer Portal website does not use tracking technologies for targeted advertising.
Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information
Your sensitive personal information will only be used or disclosed when legally permitted or required whenever you choose to limit the use of your sensitive personal information. We will place restrictions on how that information can be used or disclosed under the California privacy laws. A Limit Use request will limit the use and disclosures of your sensitive personal information subject to certain exceptions.
Partial Opt-Out: Allow for the Sale of My Professional Information but otherwise Do Not Sell My Personal Information
A Partial Opt-Out request will opt-out the personal information that we maintain under your state's privacy laws. However, information related to your occupation or profession is treated differently and may still be sold to third parties, as allowed by law.
Also, please note that an opt-out request will not apply to information that is exempt from the requirements of your state's privacy laws.
Opt-In: Allow for the Sale of My Personal Information
Under your state privacy laws, if you previously submitted an opt-out request and now want to authorize the sale or sharing of your personal information, you may submit an Opt-In request.
This applies only to personal information regulated under the state privacy laws and does not override limitations on exempt information.
Opt-In Request
I understand that by selecting the Opt-In (Allow for the Sale of My Personal Information) option I am allowing my personal information, that is maintained by LexisNexis Risk Solutions, to be sold to third parties.
Delete My Personal Information
Under the state Privacy Laws, a Delete My Personal Information request asks us to delete the personal information we maintain about you, subject to the requirements and application of your state privacy law.
Correction Request: To submit a correction request, please contact us via U.S. Mail, our Help page, or call us at 1-888-217-1591 to speak to a live LexisNexis Risk Solutions Consumer Center representative.
See more information on how LexisNexis uses your individual and professional information.

However, either your Social Security Number or your Driver’s License Number and State are required to provide a copy of your information or in support of a deletion request.
