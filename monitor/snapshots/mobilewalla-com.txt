California Residents – Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information and Limit the Use of My Sensitive Personal Information - Notices
As a California resident you have certain rights under the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), which gives California residents the right to access, port, correct, delete, or stop the “sale” or "sharing" of their personal information and limit the use of their sensitive information through a mechanism called “Do Not Sell My Personal Information” as more fully described in our Privacy Notices including our Business Services Privacy Policy, California Privacy Notice and Website Privacy Policy.
You can make a request by filling out this form Personal Information Requests Form or by emailing us at CCPACompliance@mobilewalla.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.
