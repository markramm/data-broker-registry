Removal FAQ
How long does it take to remove my information?
After following each step as outlined above and receiving confirmation that your removal request is in progress, you should anticipate your information to be removed from our site within 3 days. While it may vanish sooner, occasionally our servers require a few days to update completely.
How will I know when my request is complete?
Upon receiving your request, we will notify you that it is being processed and you will also receive a confirmation email. Kindly allow a period of 3 days for our servers to refresh. Following this, to confirm the removal, please clear your browser’s cache and then search for yourself on our site. If your information is still visible, you may need to submit another request, ensuring that the details entered in the form match the record accurately. If our system cannot match the provided information to a record, the information will not be removed.
I submitted an opt-out, but I still see my information on Google or Bing. How can I get this removed?
Should your records be deleted from our site but remain accessible through Google or Bing searches, you have the option to remove the cached information from the search engine yourself by following the instructions provided in the URLs below:
Google: https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/6349986
Bing: https://www.bing.com/webmasters/tools/contentremoval
While we automatically notify search engines of record removals from our site, we are unable to influence the speed at which Google and Bing update their caches.
Will anyone be able to see my record after I opt-out?
Your record will cease to be visible on SearchPeopleFree. However, we can only remove information from our own site. The data on our site is collected from various public record sources and publicly available information like voter records, phone directories, and property records. Removing your information from our site will not erase it from its original sources. People may still be able to find your information on other websites and through searches of government records.
Where did you get my information?
The data presented on our website is compiled from various public records and publicly accessible sources, including voter records, phone directories, and property records. Deleting your information from our site does not eliminate it from its primary sources. Your information may still be visible on other websites and through searches of government records.
